Method note: these were checked on 2026-10-17. The Rainbow Symphony (RS), 3Dstereo and American Paper Optics (APO, shop at the3dmarket.com) stores run on Shopify. For them, prices, SKUs and in-stock flags come from each store's machine-readable catalog (`/products.json`, `/collections/.../products.json`) fetched that day. These fields mirror what the product page shows: the `available` true/false flag per variant is what the page uses to show "Sold out". Berezin's figures come from its static HTML catalog page, which has no stock indicator. Amazon, Walmart and eBay pages could not be loaded. Amazon returned 503/500, Walmart served a "confirm you're human" page and eBay returned 403. The marketplace records therefore hold only the listing URL and title from search results. Their price, stock and delivery are NOT verified. Delivery times to SF 94103 are my estimates from ship-from location plus the seller's stated handling time. No seller page showed a live delivery date for zip 94103, because that needs a checkout session.

## Q4. Which sellers publish filter specs or transmission data?

### Takeaway
None of the sellers checked publishes transmission curves, dominant wavelengths or Wratten equivalents for anaglyph glasses. The most "spec-like" statements are qualitative. Plan to **measure the glasses you buy**, with a spectrometer or by photographing a projected RGB/white ramp through each lens.

### Cited Findings
- RS: "cast acrylic lenses tinted to exacting color standards". GEMS uses "film lenses". The RS catalog never mentions Wratten numbers. Its only "transmission" mentions are on eclipse/solar products — [RS catalog JSON](https://www.rainbowsymphony.com/products.json?limit=250)
- APO: "proprietary red/cyan filters", "supercharged cyan (blue) filter", TrioScopics "proprietary magenta (right) and green (left)". No numbers are given — [the3dmarket catalog](https://the3dmarket.com/products.json?limit=250)
- Berezin ProVu: "chromatically tuned to phosphors in computer screens". ProVu-X: "hybrid color cross between blue and cyan" — [Berezin](https://www.berezin.com/3d/3dglasses.htm)
- 3Dstereo describes its blue lens qualitatively as "closest to pure blue" and "Monitor blue". Its Pro-Ana line is described as "lowest ghosting". No numbers are given — [3Dstereo collection JSON](https://3dstereo.com/collections/anaglyph-glasses/products.json)
- Independent crosstalk measurements of red/cyan, blue/yellow and green/magenta glasses exist in the literature (Woods & Harris, SPIE 7524, 2010). This was found via search; the paper itself was not read — [SPIE 75240Q](https://spiedigitallibrary.org/conference-proceedings-of-spie/7524/75240Q/Comparing-levels-of-crosstalk-with-red-cyan-blue-yellow-and/10.1117/12.840835.full)

### Inferences
- The "Kodak Wratten #25-equivalent" claim mentioned in the brief could not be found on any seller page checked. Treat it as unsupported.

### Gaps
- No vendor transmission data was found. The Woods & Harris paper may contain measured spectra for specific commercial glasses, but it was not accessed.
