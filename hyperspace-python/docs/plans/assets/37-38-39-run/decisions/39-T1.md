## 1. Register-row draft

**4xx. The cut-through blend is a window's, not a ground's.**
**Answer** (controller — 2026-10-27): `render.draw_scene` picks the plain
`SRC_ALPHA, ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA` blend (alpha a² + (1 − a)·dst, the
"cut-through") only when the *window* is see-through (`Renderer.bg_alpha < 1`,
overlay mode). A scene node cleared to a zero ground always takes the
separate blend (alpha a + (1 − a)·dst), so its picture is honest
premultiplied data. That covers a scene laid into a composite's `over`
(PR #240) and one laid over the backdrop (plan 32 R).
**Built:** on an opaque window `view.cut_through` no longer changes anything.
A translucent face laid over a bright picture (a composite's `under`, or the
backdrop) covers it by its own opacity. Before, the bright picture showed
through as if the face were much fainter (alpha squared), so a box of faces
over a white or grey backdrop read as washed-out near-white. Overlay mode is
unchanged.
**Other options:** keep the squared alpha (a known lie that `cut`/`matte`
would inherit); fix only the composite path and leave the backdrop's laid-over
scene as it was (two rules for one ground).
**To reverse:** one line in `draw_scene` (the window's `bg_alpha` → the local
`_scene_ground()`). Cheap; the two backdrop fixtures' goldens would move back.
**Goldens re-recorded (orchestrator decision 9):** eight frames of two test
fixtures, `backdrop-still` and `media-chain-backdrop` (`present` wiring:
scene, scene+through, scene+hands, scene+hands+through). Reason: a scene
laid over the backdrop also clears to a zero ground (plan 32 R), which the
spike missed. The old frames squared the faces' alpha and washed them toward
white over the backdrop; the new frames are the premultiplied over. No
factory preset moved. `GOLDENS_DIGEST` was re-pinned (commit 10968e4).
**Rough edge:** in overlay mode (a see-through window) with cut-through on,
a laid-over scene (over the backdrop, or in a composite's `over`) still takes
the plain blend on its zero ground, so its alpha is still squared there. That
follows the spec ("only when the window is see-through"), and it is what
`cut`/`matte` will inherit in overlay. AJ.10 is still unchecked by a person.
**Judge it:** AJ.10.
