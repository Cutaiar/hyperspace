## 1. Register-row drafts (the docs task numbers them in 430–439)

**4xx. What Drop's one undo step puts back, and what it leaves**
- **Answer** (controller — 2026-10-27): Drop is one `Undo.revert` to the hold point's checkpoint, cut down to the preset's part (`hold._preset_part`). It puts back:
  - the performance values
  - the graph (the preset's section and, through the show's mark, the rig's bus cables)
  - the routes
  - the desk's preset layer
  - the macros
  - the loaded preset's name, and with it the dirty dot. If TUMBLE was clean at HOLD, it is TUMBLE and clean after Drop, even if TRIP was loaded and edited in between.

  Drop leaves the rig's params (`eye.*`, output, hands feel, tempo) as they are now. One ⌘Z after Drop brings back the whole dropped state: the other preset, its edits, its dirty dot and the graph. The rig's params are untouched by both steps.
- **Built:** after DROP, the strip names the preset the projector is showing, and its dot is as it was at HOLD. One ⌘Z returns to exactly where the operator was before Drop.
- **Other options:** Drop could restore only the held params and the wiring, without the name, desk, routes or macros. The canvas would then name one preset while the projector showed another.
- **To reverse:** moderate. Narrow `_preset_part`, or skip the marks in `Undo.revert`.
- **Rough edge:** a desk learn made during the hold (say `output.take`, learned while held) goes into the preset layer, so Drop takes it back too. That may surprise someone who learned a button mid-hold. ⌘Z restores it.
- **Judge it:** AI.4.

No other choices were made: the tests pin the spec as built.

## 2. Hand-check drafts (group AI, B4 numbers them 377–383)

**AI.1 Hold keeps the projector while a new preset is started** (the rig, projector up, GRAPH open, music playing)
Headless: `tests/test_hold.py::test_on_gl_the_program_draws_the_held_wiring_while_a_graph_edit_lands`, `::test_on_gl_the_projector_keeps_the_held_frame_while_the_preview_shows_the_edit`, `::test_the_program_draws_the_held_look_and_the_live_one_comes_back`, `::test_held_the_outputs_draw_the_program_and_the_preview_follows`.
1. Load a moving look.
2. Press **hold**, the first chip on GRAPH's head.
3. Press **new** on the PRESET strip.

*Pass:* the projector keeps the old look and keeps moving with the music. GRAPH's backdrop goes black. The PROJECTOR inset, framed in red in the canvas corner, shows the old look, and the head shows the red `HELD 0:0x — …` line. *Fail:* the projector goes black, which points to `Hold.program` not being around `draw_outputs` in `app._draw_render`. Or the inset is missing, which points to `monitor` not being wanted, or to output 1 not publishing into it.

**AI.2 Building a scene during a hold shows only behind GRAPH** (same)

**AI.3 Take cuts the preview onto the projector** (same, look-back on)

## 3. As-built notes (B2)

- **What was built:** three tests in `tests/test_hold.py`, which now has 22.
  - **`test_on_gl_the_program_draws_the_held_wiring_while_a_graph_edit_lands`** (`empty_frame`, GL; the prompt's (a)).
    - W1, today's frame as a wired section, is landed through a real `preset.GraphSection` on a real `Renderer`.
    - With the hold engaged, the GRAPH page's own edit (`graphview.Edit(graph=gs).insert_effect(("scene", "output.all.image"), "kaleido")`) lands W2.
    - The outputs' frame matches W1's frame drawn alone before the hold, byte for byte.
    - The preview, drawn through `Hold.draw_preview` and `preview_renderer`, matches W2's frame drawn alone on the main renderer after Take, byte for byte.
    - W1's and W2's frames differ.
  - **`test_drop_after_a_whole_new_look_is_one_undo_step_back_and_one_forward`** (headless over `test_undo.boot`; the prompt's (b)).
    - During the hold: three params moved, a GRAPH landing, TRIP loaded, its macros set and a route removed, and `eye.fov` moved.
    - After Drop: `same_look` (via `test_undo.look`) matches the hold point, and so does the wiring. The preset is TUMBLE with its dot clean, and `eye.fov` keeps its new value. Exactly one step was pushed (`"drop the preview"`), and nothing re-lands after settling.
    - After one undo: TRIP is back, edited and dirty, with the edited wiring. `eye.fov` is still kept, and the stack is back at its pre-Drop depth.
  - **`test_learned_desk_buttons_light_while_held`** (check 5): a real `Learn` through `MidiIO` onto APC40 notes 0x3C and 0x3D on channel 0, which are free on the startup desk. The LEDs are read from the fake out port.
- **What differs from the dispatch:**
  - The preview in the GL test runs with its clock pinned (`pr.now = lambda t_now=None: T`), because `Hold.draw_preview` passes no `t_now`. Without the pin, the preview could not be compared byte for byte with a reference frame. The override is on the instance only; no seam was added to `hold.py`.
  - The "drawn alone" references are the same renderers, before the hold and after Take, rather than fresh ones. A fresh renderer would need its own registry and `FxState`, and kaleido's instance params live on the shared ones.
  - Check 5 learns two buttons, hold and take, as the spec says. Drop is fired from the registry instead, because the third free-looking note (0x3E) belongs to `ui.cheatsheet`.
- **Not verified by any person:** everything in AI.1–AI.7 on the machine. That means the inset, the red line, the forced backdrop, trails through a cut, the FPS, Syphon/NDI, the real LEDs, and the feel of a pumping held param.
- **Rough edges:** trail continuity through Take (AI.3) has no headless test. The look-back is off by default in the test, and a meaningful check would need several frames of history, which makes it a golden-style test and not a cheap one here.
