## 1. Register-row drafts (numbered 4xx; the docs task renumbers inside 417–429)

**4xa — A thumbnail is 192 texels on its long side, made by one 4×4-tap downsample pass.**
**Answer** (controller — 2026-10-27): `outputs.THUMB_PX = 192`, the picture's
aspect (`thumb_size`); one draw per node of `THUMB_FS`, sixteen bilinear taps
over one thumbnail texel's footprint (each tap averages 2×2 source texels, so
a 1920-wide picture's ten texels a thumbnail texel are read eight across),
through a sampler of its own, written opaque (the picture over black).
**Built**: every picture node on GRAPH shows a small, smooth, live picture;
see-through layers read as they would on a black window.
**Other options**: mipmaps (P1's word) — `build_mipmaps` on a frame-owned
texture switches its min filter to mipmapped, which every later sample of
that texture would see: a golden risk, and a mip chain on a 1080p target
every frame per node; `copy_framebuffer` (a nearest blit: aliases badly at
10:1); a second, chained ½-size pass.
**To reverse**: one shader string and one constant in `outputs.py`.
**Rough edge**: at 10:1 the 8-of-10 coverage can shimmer on fine line art
(the shape's edges); a picture's alpha is dropped on the thumbnail.
**Judge it**: AH.1.

**4xb — Dropped after a second unwanted; dimmed, then black a second after it made nothing.**
**Answer** (controller — 2026-10-27): `THUMB_DROP_S = 1.0` (a name not
wanted for a second is forgotten, its texture freed); `THUMB_BLACK_S = 1.0`;
`PreviewTextures.shade`: 1 copied the renderer's last frame, `DIM = 0.45`
while the last copy is under a second old, 0 after (a number the page
multiplies the picture by, never a copy).
**Built**: a node that stops drawing (switched off behind a switch, a hand
leaves, unwired) goes grey at once and black after a second; panning a
node off screen frees its texture a second later.
**Other options**: hold the last picture forever; drop at once (a flicker
while panning); a longer keep.
**To reverse**: three constants.
**Rough edge**: a node wanted for the first time shows a black box for one
frame.
**Judge it**: AH.1, AH.5.

**4xc — How a node that makes no picture shows one: one table.**
**Answer** (controller — 2026-10-27): `graphview.THUMBS` (kind → how):
`picture` for every picture kind (channel, pattern, scene, backdrop,
composite, switch, delay, resize, lookback, echo, dream, effect, copy, hands
layer, guide); `scene` for the shape and a cloud (the first scene that draws
it, at `ui_graph.FAINT_PIC = 0.4`); `layer` for a hands node (the first
hands layer drawing it, else the scene it is in, not faint); `window` for an
output (output 1 the page's existing `Preview`, wanted only while its node
is on screen; any other the `Handoff.frame` its presenter is handed — a
texture of the render context, no readback, the hands not laid on — or a
publish-only output's own target). All outputs, the camera, ground and the
whole modulation side stay text. A kind the table does not name whose node's
`OUTPUT` is `IMAGE` (plan 39's `segment`, `matte`, `cut`) is a `picture`
(`thumb_of`) — the seam, with no code for plan 39.
**Built**: outputs 2–4 show what their window gets (before the hands are
laid on); a shape shows its scene faint.
**Other options**: output N≥2 by the end's frame texture (pre-fold); All
outputs as its `image` wire's picture.
**To reverse**: table entries in `graphview.py` and the matching branch in
`render.thumb_source`.
**Rough edge**: output 1's picture never dims (the `Preview` has no age: a
frame that fails leaves its last picture lit — recorded, not built);
output 2+ thumbnails lack the hands and readout the window lays over; an output painted direct (output 1 only, in practice) has no
`Handoff`, hence the Preview.
**Judge it**: AH.1.

**4xd — `PreviewTextures` lives in `outputs.py`, beside `Preview`; the
resolution in `render.py`.**
**Answer** (controller — 2026-10-27): the texture keeper and its pass are a
control-window tap like `Preview` and know nothing of the graph, so they sit
beside it (still importing only moderngl and glsl); what each wanted name's
picture is (`thumb_source`, over `graph.picture`) is the renderer's, called
from `Renderer._thumbnails` at the end of `_for_the_panel`. The renderer
holds one (`Renderer.thumbs`, made again after a hot reload); the page
reaches it as `panel.renderer.thumbs` (no `app.py` change).
**Built**: nothing to see.
**Other options**: all of it in `render.py`.
**To reverse**: a move.
**Rough edge**: none known.
**Judge it**: —.

**4xe — The cost per kind, and how the ask stays out of the frame.**
**Answer** (controller — 2026-10-27): a node that already made a texture
this frame — the scene, a hands layer, a channel's fit, the pattern, a
look-back's trail or a dream bound by a consumer, an effect held for two
readers or folded after the scene, a composite made into a texture, a
window's handoff — costs **one blit** (192-texel pass). A node that painted
straight into its consumer — an effect with one reader, a switch, a delay
or echo laid by cover, a resize, a composite painted direct, a look-back
painted direct — is **painted again** at the frame's size into a texture of
its own (`graph.picture`'s `#picture` frame target, or a pool texture for an
on effect), **then blitted**: a full-size pass plus the blit. Only wanted
(on-screen, drawn) nodes are asked; nothing of any kind behind an unpicked
switch input (`Graph.pulled`, checked before anything is read — review fix:
it first gated only made nodes and effects, so a composite behind an
unpicked input was painted and read its unpicked channel), nothing that
reads nothing (`Node.reads`). A repainted node holds a full-size frame
target while wanted — about 8 MB per node at 1080p RGBA8 (a half-float
`#fit` or trail target twice that). SYSTEM says the repaints:
`thumbnails · N copied · K repainted · M kept`. During the asks
the GPU timer is muted (`PassTimer._active`) and the frame's `stats` are
restored after, so the SYSTEM page's per-node times and counts stay the
frame's.
**Built**: GRAPH open with twenty nodes on screen costs twenty small passes
plus a repaint for each painted-direct node.
**Other options**: paint at the thumbnail's size (`Frame.at`): cheaper, but
a see-through ground and `u_opaque` 0 — a different picture.
When anything was copied, `_thumbnails` flushes the render context
(`glFlush`, the call app.py's `_gl_flush` makes; not `ctx.finish`), so the
control window never samples a thumbnail before its pass is submitted —
app.py flushes only with a second output or the backdrop wanted.
**To reverse**: per kind, a branch in `thumb_source`.
**Rough edge**: each repainted node keeps a full-size frame target (or one
more effects-pool texture) while it is wanted (`_prune_sized` frees an idle
frame target after its keep); a look-back asked after the frame's capture
shows this frame's commit too, and an echo asked after the capture whose
slot was just rewritten reads nothing (dim).
**Judge it**: AH.2.

**4xf — A picture node's box is its node's width at 16:9, whatever the picture.**
**Answer** (controller — 2026-10-27): `graphview.THUMB_H = 134` canvas units
(190 × 1.25 × 9/16); the picture is fitted whole inside (`backdrop_rect`,
letterboxed on black). `graphview.height` counts it, collapsed (title +
box: the N7 lines leave the body) and expanded (title + box + ports).
**Built**: a collapsed picture node is a title and a picture; the layout
never moves when a picture's size changes.
**Other options**: the box at the picture's own aspect (a portrait channel
then pushes its column down, and the layout moves when a window resizes).
**To reverse**: `thumb` height in `graphview.height` and `_thumb`.
**Rough edge**: a collapsed picture node lost its N7 lines (switch pick,
"reaches nothing", `said`) until C2's hover carries them; a faint node's
title is still faint. Auto-laid positions of picture nodes moved once
(their height changed); a preset's stored positions do not move.
**Judge it**: AH.3.

## 3. As-built notes for C1

**As built.**
- `outputs.PreviewTextures` (beside `Preview`): `want(ids)` (names → how,
  or names), `take`, `begin_frame`, `put(name, src)` (one `THUMB_FS` pass
  into a `thumb_size` texture), `end_frame` (drops names unwanted for
  `THUMB_DROP_S`), `shade`, `get`, `size_of`, `count`, `kept`, `close`.
  With no context it keeps only names, sizes and times (the page's tests).
- `render.thumb_source(frame, name, how, pulled)` — the resolver for the
  four hows — and `Renderer._thumbnails`, called last in `_for_the_panel`,
  from the frame `_draw_frame` keeps on `_thumb_frame` after a successful
  `evaluate` (cleared there). Timer muted, `stats` restored in place.
  `Renderer.thumbs` made in `__init__`, closed and set None on `on_reload`,
  made again by `_thumbs` / `_thumbnails`.
- `graphview.THUMBS`, `thumb_of`, `THUMB_FAINT`, `THUMB_H`, `PICTURE` /
  `SCENE_PIC` / `LAYER_PIC` / `WINDOW_PIC`; `VNode.thumb` (set in `build`),
  `VNode.picture` (set by the page each frame); `height` counts the box.
- `ui_graph`: `thumb_name`, `by_preview`, `on_screen`, `thumb_wants`,
  `_want_thumbs` (after the nodes are drawn: their positions and sizes are
  the editor's), `_body_of`, `_thumb`, `thumbnail_line`, `FAINT_PIC`;
  `_canvas` keeps the visible canvas rect (`st["seen_rect"]`) and the
  sources (`st["thumbs"]`, `st["preview"]`). `_node` draws the box after
  the title, collapsed or expanded; a picture node's summary lines are not
  drawn. `_head`, `head_line`, chips, `COLOURS`, the backdrop code: untouched.
- `ui.py`: the SYSTEM line under the GRAPH page's own timing line.
- Tests: `tests/test_thumbnails.py` (14); `test_graph_legible.py`'s height
  assertion changed (a picture node's collapsed height is its box's; a
  device's still its lines').

**Differs from the spec.**
- No mipmaps: a 16-tap downsample pass (row 4xa) — P1 says "mipmapped".
- The box is fixed 16:9 at the node's width with the picture fitted inside
  (row 4xf) — the picture keeps its aspect, the node does not take it.
- Output 1 is the page's `Preview` (wanted while its node is on screen),
  not a thumbnail; outputs 2–4 their `Handoff.frame` (no hands on).

**Not verified by a person.** Everything visual: how the thumbnails read at
the canvas's zooms, the faint shape, the dim/black ages, the cost on the
show Mac with GRAPH open (AH.1–AH.3, AH.5), and that the control window's
context sees this frame's thumbnails (`_thumbnails` flushes after copying).

**Known rough edges.** A first-wanted node is black for one frame; the
collapsed picture node lost its N7 lines until C2 (the hover); a look-back
or echo asked after the frame's capture is one commit ahead (or dim); each
painted-direct node wanted keeps a full-size target while wanted; a channel
that no wire reaches is a black box (nothing built for it); the headless
canvas in the tests is narrow, so the page test pans to the scene's column.
