## 1. Register-row drafts (numbered 4xx; the docs task places them in 440–459)

### 4xa. A new matte passes the bright half, hard-edged: luma, key 0.5, soft 0, no invert, no feather; the chroma key starts on green

- **Answer.** controller — 2026-10-27. A new node has no "today's picture" to keep; the defaults are the ones that do something on any plain picture the moment it is wired.
- **Built.** `view.MatteState()`: `matte.from` luma, `matte.key` 0.5, `matte.soft` 0, `matte.invert` off, `matte.feather` 0, `matte.hue` 1/3 (green), `matte.sat` 1. Group `matte`, the preset's, every param modulatable. Ranges: key 0–1, soft 0–0.5, feather 0–32 px, hue and sat 0–1.
- **Other options.** Key 0.0, so the whole picture passes until a knob moves. Chroma as the default source (a green screen). A small default feather, so edges are never jagged.
- **To reverse.** Change one dataclass default. A saved preset carries its own values, so only new mattes change.
- **Rough edge.** Luma at 0.5 on a dark live camera can pass nothing, and the output is black until `key` comes down.
- **Judge it.** AJ.5, AJ.6.

### 4xb. The promotion is a render node per mask, `<mask>(picture)`: RGBA8, opaque grey, at the consumer's size, the mask fitted by cover

- **Answer.** controller — 2026-10-27. W1 asks for "white where it passes, black elsewhere, at the consumer's size, fitted by cover". `graph.Node.__init__` type-checks every port, so a mask cannot be handed straight to a picture port.
- **Built.** `_Build._taken` reads a mask wired into an `IMAGE` port (every `_from`, an effect's `aux`, each window's ports) through one `graph.MaskPicture` per mask node, named `matte(picture)`. It is a render node only: no GRAPH node, no wire of its own, untimed. `paint` runs `masks.PROMOTE_FS` into the consumer's viewport: white (1,1,1,1) where the mask passes, opaque black (0,0,0,1) elsewhere. `image` paints that once a frame into an RGBA8 `_frame_target` at the frame's size. A mask port (`lookback.cover`) takes the mask itself. The mask is R8 (one channel, 8-bit), linear-filtered, at its maker's size.
- **Other options.** Make the consumer ask the mask (`Node.picture_of_mask`) at every picture-port read site. Give each mask node a picture-shaped `image()`, which a mask port would then have to undo.
- **To reverse.** Delete `_taken` and `MaskPicture`. The type rule then refuses mask → picture again.
- **Rough edge.** "The consumer's size" is the frame's (or `Frame.at`'s for the faces). A window of another shape shows the frame by its own fit, as every picture does. While the mask reads nothing, the promoted picture is the ground `(0,0,0,bg_alpha)`, not opaque black.
- **Judge it.** AJ.6.

### 4xc. The feather is two Gaussian passes (across, then down) through an R8 half-way texture, up to 65 taps each

- **Answer.** controller — 2026-10-27. W2 says "two chained passes inside the node, separable".
- **Built.** `masks.BLUR_FS`: radius = `matte.feather` px (capped at 32), sigma = radius / 2, taps out to the radius, each held inside the edge texels. The first pass goes into `(<name>#mask/blur, size)` and the second back into `(<name>#mask, size)`. Both run only when feather > 0. Cost: two one-channel passes at the mask's size. At 720p and 32 px that is ≤ 65 texture reads per pixel per pass. It has not been measured on the show Mac.
- **Other options.** A box blur, or a mipmap-based blur, which are cheaper at large radii. Feather by distance (a dilate) rather than by blur.
- **To reverse.** Swap the shader. The texture keys stay.
- **Rough edge.** The blur is quantised to 8 bits (R8 targets), so a wide feather's tail steps in 1/255. A feather also lets a little of a hard mask's "pass" leak out past the key's edge (it widens both ways).
- **Judge it.** AJ.5 (feather by eye).

### 4xd. Chroma is the distance between HSV chroma vectors; luma is Rec. 709 on the premultiplied colour

- **Answer.** controller — 2026-10-27. The spec says "distance from `matte.hue` at `matte.sat`". These are the readings chosen inside that.
- **Built.** `MATTE_FS`. **luma**: `dot(rgb, (0.2126, 0.7152, 0.0722))` of the premultiplied colour, so a transparent pixel is dark. **alpha**: the picture's own `a`. **chroma**: the pixel's colour is unpremultiplied and taken to HSV, its chroma vector is `s·(cos 2πh, sin 2πh)`, and `v = (1 − min(|c − k|, 1))·a`, where k is the key's vector `sat·(cos 2πhue, sin 2πhue)`. In every mode the pixel passes where `v ≥ key`; `soft` > 0 gives a smoothstep `key ± soft` instead.
- **Other options.** Luma of the unpremultiplied colour. YCbCr or Lab distance for chroma. A separate tolerance knob in place of `key`.
- **To reverse.** The shader alone. The params keep their meaning.
- **Rough edge.** Chroma ignores value, so a dark green keys like a bright one. Greys sit at the wheel's centre and pass only when `sat` is low.
- **Judge it.** AJ.5 (chroma on a green-screen still, if one is at hand).

## 3. As-built notes (T2)

**Built, in the plan's terms.**
- **The mask wire (W1).** `graph.MASK` stays the type; its comment now says what a mask is. `_wired_error` accepts a `MASK` maker into an `IMAGE` port and refuses a picture into a mask port with `PICTURE_INTO_MASK`. The build reads each such mask through one `MaskPicture` per mask node (row 4xb). `scene.cover → lookback.cover` is unchanged, with no promotion between (row 4xe). Every factory preset's `graph.signature` is unchanged (`test_graph_frame.py`, all cases green).
- **`matte[.n]` (W2).** `MatteAt` and its reader in `NODE_KINDS`, in `DECLARED` and `RESERVED`, in `_wired_kind` (`_MATTE`), `WIRED_MAKES` (MASK), `WIRED_TAKES` (`{"image": IMAGE}`), `_WireCtx.mattes`, `GraphSpec.mattes` and the `_Build._make` branch. A matte fed by nothing is not built and is dormant in words; one whose input reads nothing makes no mask and says so. `MatteNode` follows `ResizeNode`'s pattern: pulled once a frame into `(<name>#mask, size)` at its input's own size (else the frame's), timed under its own name, `said` like "64×48 mask · luma at 0.50 · feather 8 px". *(Review fix.)* `own_size` passes its input's own size through, as `Effect.own_size` does: it is None when the input has none, which keeps `test_nothing_of_plan_33_has_an_own_size` true. The mask is made at that size. A mask port binds `MatteNode.image`, which is the mask refitted by cover to the frame's size (`MaskPasses.fit`, one R8 pass, `<name>#fit`), because `lookback.cover` fetches its mask 1:1 by the frame's pixel. The promotion (`MaskPicture`, through `native`) and `CUT_FS` (now with `u_scale = cover_scale(mask, picture)`) fit the native mask by cover themselves. So a mask lands in the same place on every consumer.
- **`masks.py` (new, a leaf).** `MATTE_FS`, `BLUR_FS`, `PROMOTE_FS` and `CUT_FS` (written in full with a `cut()` method and a test, ready for T3), plus `MaskPasses`, which compiles the four programs once and asks its one-channel targets of the renderer's `_frame_target`. Every pass samples inside the edge texels (never wrapped). `cover_scale` is held equal to `media_tex.fit_scale(COVER)` by a test.
- **`render.py`, the hand-off only.** `Renderer.mask_passes()` (lazy, kept) and a `components` argument on `_frame_target` (4 by default; a stored target of another component count is made again).
- **Params.** `params.declare_mattes` on `declare_resizes`' pattern (ids `matte[.n].{from,key,soft,invert,feather,hue,sat}`; `from` is stored as `MatteState.source`), plus `is_matte_id`, `matte_ids` and `MATTE_GROUP`. `matte` sits in `PERFORMANCE_GROUPS` after `resize`. `build_registry` binds `ViewState.mattes`. `Frame.mattes` is a property reading the view state, so `render.draw_outputs` is untouched.
- **Preset.** `GraphSection.land` declares the mattes beside the resizes. A load defers `matte.*` values until the graph has landed (as a resize's), a new preset leaves them out, and `NO_MODULE` includes the group. The `/7` round trip of a `{"kind": "matte"}` entry and its values is tested (`test_the_fixture_saves_and_loads_as_seven`). The schema is unchanged.
- **GRAPH.** Table entries only (row 4xi).
- **Fixture.** `tests/fixtures/presets/mask-as-picture.json` (`/7`) has 8 new goldens, one PNG. Golden count: 757 → 765, none moved. `GOLDENS_SINCE` and the count in `test_golden_frames.py` are updated, as is `test_graph_free`'s schema ladder (now `/2`–`/7`).

**Differs from the brief or spec, and why.**
- There is no frame-signature case for the fixture (row 4xh).
- `masks` is cold for hot reload (row 4xg). `hotreload.py` is plan 38's.
- A promoted cable is drawn red (by its port's type), not purple into a red port. Fixing it needs a graphview change outside the table entries, which is T5's (row 4xi).
- There is no picker item or `render_add` branch for the matte; T5 adds them (row 4xi).
- `tests/test_hotreload.py`, `tests/test_plan36_edges.py`, `tests/test_golden_frames.py` and `tests/test_graph_free.py` were each changed with one stated reason (above). `hyperspace/ui_presets.py` got a one-word change outside the ownership table (row 4xj).

**Not verified by any person.** Every picture is a test's or a golden's. Nobody has looked at a matte at the machine, the blur's cost on the show Mac is unmeasured, and chroma has not been tried on a real green screen.

**Known rough edges.** A matte cannot be added from the GRAPH picker until T5. The promoted cable is red. `scene.cover` cannot be seen on a window. The feather is 8-bit. The promoted picture of a mask reading nothing is the ground, not opaque black.
