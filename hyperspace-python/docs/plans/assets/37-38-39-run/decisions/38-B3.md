## Register-row drafts

**4xx. A landing while held keeps the held program's nodes** (C1, ruling 12)
- **Answer** (controller — 2026-10-27): `GraphSection._settle` asks the hold's `keep(kind, names)` for each kind it settles (`fx`, `switch`, `resize`, `lookback`). While held, that returns the landed names plus the program's, snapshotted at engage (`GraphSection.names()`). Take releases the hook and settles to the live graph. Drop releases it before its revert lands the hold point.
- **Built:** with the projector held, pressing `new`, loading another preset, deleting an effect or switch on GRAPH, or ⌘Z leaves the projector's look whole: effects still drawn, switch picks kept, effect clocks running on. After Take the program's effects are gone if the new look lacks them. If the new look has them, their clocks carry on mid-stride.
- **Other options:** a second `FxState` and registry slice for the program (large). Or refuse landings while held (defeats the point).
- **To reverse:** cheap. The hook defaults to none, and removing the hold removes it.
- **Rough edge:** while held, the kept instances' params still exist in the registry, so they show on the EFFECTS and desk pages and can be learned. GRAPH does not draw them (they are not in the live spec). An effect deleted during the hold and added again before Take gets the program's kept values, not its defaults (M-d). The program graph rebuilds whenever a landing changes the effect set (the `fx.specs` key), but losslessly now. That corrects B1's as-built claim that "the program's graph never rebuilds while held" (M2).
- **Judge it:** AI.1, AI.2.

**4xx. The program's kept params are not the preset's**
- **Answer** (controller — 2026-10-27): `Presets._values` leaves out `Hold.program_only()` (the kept instances the live graph lacks). This goes beyond ruling 12's letter: it is one more expression in `preset.py`, outside the land block.
- **Built:** saving during a hold writes only the live graph's values. The dirty dot does not light, or go out, for the projector's leftovers, nor change at Take.
- **Other options:** let a save write them (the next load would carry values for nodes the graph lacks: dead ids in the owner's library).
- **To reverse:** cheap. Drop the filter.
- **Rough edge:** none known.
- **Judge it:** AI.2 (save during a hold, then reload).

**4xx. A load's delay reset while held is the preview's** (I1)
- **Answer** (controller — 2026-10-27): `Presets.load`'s R19 reset goes to the hold while held (`GraphSection.hold.reset_delays`). That resets the preview renderer's delays and marks the hold "loaded". At Take, if a load happened, the projector's renderer is reset.
- **Built:** loading during a hold no longer cuts the projector's echo or delay trails. The preview starts empty, as a load should. Take after a load starts the projector's delays empty. Take without one keeps them.
- **Other options:** never reset at Take (the old preset's trail would bleed into the new look).
- **To reverse:** cheap.
- **Rough edge:** a load made before the preview renderer exists (GRAPH never opened) resets nothing until Take.
- **Judge it:** AI.3.

**4xx. The canvas reads the preview's graph while held** (I2, ruling 13)
- **Answer** (controller — 2026-10-27): `hold.canvas_renderer(panel)` returns the preview renderer while held and drawn, otherwise the panel's own. These read `_graph` through it: `ui_graph`'s `view_of`, `render_edit` and `_edit`, and `ui.py`'s `graph_words` and `dormant_lines`.
- **Built:** while held, nodes added on GRAPH get real refs, and the dormant and refusal lines describe the preview (what TAKE puts up), not the projector.
- **Other options:** none worth it.
- **To reverse:** cheap.
- **Rough edge:** until the preview has been drawn once (the first frame with GRAPH showing), the canvas reads the held graph. **Plan 37's thumbnails (`thumbs.want` / `_thumb_frame`) must route through the same helper at integration (the controller does this at the 37 merge).**
- **Judge it:** AI.2.

**4xx. The preview drawn with output 1's switches** (M3)
- **Answer** (controller — 2026-10-27): output 1's mutes and flips, with dim forced to 1.0.
- **Built:** the backdrop while held is oriented and muted like the unheld backdrop (output 1), without the room's dim.
- **Other options:** `Switches()` defaults (the first cut), or output 1's dim too.
- **To reverse:** one line in `app._draw_preview`.
- **Rough edge:** a flip set for a Pepper's Ghost glass shows mirrored on the laptop. The unheld backdrop already does this.

**4xx. The turn's speed and planes are one for both** (M1)
- **Answer:** `view.speed` and `view.plane.*` are out of the held set (`ONE_FOR_BOTH`). The integrator reads them once a frame for both renderers.
- **Built:** turning speed or plane knobs during a hold no longer counts on the red line. It does visibly change the projector's turn, as D2 already says.
- **To reverse:** cheap. **Judge it:** AI.7.

**4xx. Drop also reverts what the show's mark carries** (M4, for B4)
- Drop's undo revert puts back the show's mark: the show itself (if one was opened during the hold), the GRAPH layout of rig nodes, and the rig's bus cables. A desk learn made during the hold goes back with it too, through the preset's desk layer. This is consistent with "everything since HOLD that is the look", but opening a show during a hold and then dropping reopens the earlier show. **Other option:** exclude the show mark from `_preset_part` (moderate). **Judge it:** AI.4.

**4xx. The preview renderer's lifetime and hot reload** (M6, for B4)
- The preview renderer is made on first use and kept for the session after Take (its GL objects stay). It is a hot-reload target, but its sub-objects (its `Fx` chain, look-back, ghosts) are not, so a reload of `fx.py` updates them only on the main renderer. **Other option:** release it at Take or Drop, or add its sub-objects to the targets list (small). **Judge it:** AI.6.

**4xx. The PROJECTOR inset draws on the foreground list** (M7, for B4)
- `_monitor` draws on imgui's foreground draw list, so the inset sits over popups and menus opened on the canvas (the node picker, context menus) in its corner. Moving it to the window's draw list would put it under the node editor's child canvas. Not fixed. **Other option:** draw it inside the editor's canvas as a suspended overlay (moderate). **Judge it:** AI.1 (open the node picker near the top-right while held).

**4xx. The plan 39 seam: no segment workers in the preview** (ruling 13)
- The preview renderer gets no segment workers. A `segment` node in the preview passes through and says "no worker in the preview". `Renderer.close` closes only the workers it made (the preview shares media, hands, look-back and fx state with the main renderer), and the app's close list adds the preview's close. Plan 39 T4 builds this. Plan 38 changes nothing for it. **Judge it:** AJ checks with a hold on.

## Verification follow-up (c834e74)

**4xx. An undo mark made while held records the landed graph's own instances** (I-a)
- **Answer** (controller — 2026-10-27): `GraphSection.mark()` records `frozenset(spec.effect_names())` while a hold is on and a spec has landed, not `FxState.held` (which then includes the program's kept instances). Unheld the two are equal (plan 36 T3), so nothing changes outside a hold.
- **Built:** after Take, ⌘Z of a step made during the hold lands exactly that step's graph and its effects. No program-only effect comes back, and a save carries no dead id.
- **Other options:** have `put` clip `mark[3]` to the landed spec's names when no hold is on (equivalent; the mark-side fix keeps `put` simple).
- **To reverse:** cheap.
- **Rough edge:** none known.
- **Judge it:** AI.4.

**What plan 39 must add at integration (M-a)**, so its mattes, cuts and segments are kept by a hold and left out of a save. Line numbers are on c834e74:
1. `GraphSection.names` in `hyperspace/preset.py`: a key per kind in **both** branches. The no-spec branch (~449) gets `"matte": [], "cut": [], "segment": []`. The spec branch (~450–453) gets `"matte": [m.name for m in spec.mattes]`, and the same for `cuts` and `segments`, using whatever attribute names T2–T4 give `GraphSpec`.
2. `GraphSection._settle`'s no-spec dict (~466–467): the same three keys, empty.
3. The `declare_mattes` / `declare_cuts` / `declare_segments` calls go **inside `_settle`** beside `declare_lookbacks` (~482), reading `names["matte"]` and the like. They must not go in `land`, or a hold will not keep them.
4. `hold.PROGRAM_IDS` in `hyperspace/hold.py`: `"matte"`, `"cut"` and `"segment"`, each mapping `(reg, name)` to that instance's param ids (`matte_ids(name)` or the equivalent helper). Without these, `Hold.program_only` raises `KeyError` the first time a held matte, cut or segment is deleted. That loud failure is intended, so the gap cannot pass silently into a save.
5. `Hold.keep` needs nothing: it is generic over list and set kinds.
