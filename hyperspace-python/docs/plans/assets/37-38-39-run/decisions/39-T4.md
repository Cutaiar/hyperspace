### 4xr. A new segment's values add nothing to the model's own matte

- **Answer.** controller, 2026-10-27. A new node has no "today's picture" to keep, so each default is the reading that changes nothing about what the model gives.
- **Built.** `view.SegmentState()` holds the defaults. All params are in group `segment`, the preset's.

  | Param | Default | Range | Modulatable |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `segment.mode` | person | person / motion / subject | no |
  | `segment.quality` | fast | fast / balanced / accurate | no |
  | `segment.feather` | 0 px | 0–32 | yes |
  | `segment.smooth` | 0 | 0–1 | yes |
  | `segment.on` | on | on / off | yes |
  | `segment.sense` | 0.1 | 0–1 | yes |
  | `segment.learn` | 3 s | 0.1–30 | yes |

  `mode` and `quality` are not modulatable because a lane must not restart a model or swap a request each frame. The spec names `feather`, `smooth` and `on` as modulatable. `sense` and `learn` are modulatable too: they are only uniforms.
- **Other options.**
  - A small default feather (2 px) to hide the 256×192 matte's steps.
  - A default `smooth` of about 0.3 against flicker.
  - Making `quality` modulatable.
- **To reverse.** Change one dataclass default, or one `modulatable=` flag. A saved preset carries its own values.
- **Rough edge.** The fast matte at feather 0 can show a soft, slightly stepped edge on a projector. Nobody has looked at it yet (AJ.3).
- **Judge it.** AJ.2, AJ.3.

### 4xs. The node says `<w>×<h> · <backend> <level> · a frame or two late · <n> fps`, where the rate is results a second over the last 2 s

- **Answer.** controller, 2026-10-27. This is the spec's wording. The rate is the worker's.
- **Built.** The `said` line comes from `segment.SegmentWorker.said()`:
  - **Normal running.** `256×144 · Vision fast · a frame or two late · 29 fps`. The size is the matte as uploaded: Vision's 256×192, resampled to the frame's aspect (row 4xt). The fps is `(n − 1) / span` over the results of the last `RATE_SPAN` (2 s).
  - **Starting up.** `loading Vision …` while the backend opens. `waiting for Vision fast` before the first result.
  - **Passing through.** In each of the cases below the node adds ": its picture through, whole" (and " until the first matte" while it waits):
    - an error: `Vision: <the error>`;
    - a missing library: the reason string;
    - off: `off: … the worker idle`;
    - `no frame on channel a`;
    - `no worker in the preview`;
    - subject: `subject is not in this build …`.
  - **Motion.** `64×48 · motion · sense 0.10 · learns in 3 s`.
  - Drops are counted (`worker.drops`) but not said. With latest-wins they are normal whenever the model is slower than the camera.
- **Other options.**
  - Say the model's raw size (256×192) rather than the resampled one.
  - Say the drops.
  - Say the latency in frames, as the dream's status says "N behind".
- **To reverse.** Edit one method's f-string.
- **Rough edge.** The node's text shows 256×144 where the spec says 256×192.
- **Judge it.** AJ.2.

### 4xt. Vision's matte is resampled to the frame's aspect in the worker; the matte is the worker's array as it is, rows top first, flagged

- **Answer.** controller, 2026-10-27, from what the spike showed when run again on a 640×360 frame. Vision's person matte has a fixed size per level (256×192, 512×384, 2016×1512), always 4:3, and it is stretched over the whole image. Every mask consumer fits by cover, so a 4:3 matte over a 16:9 picture would land wrong.
- **Built.**
  - `VisionBackend.run` resamples the matte bilinearly (PIL, on the worker's thread) to the frame's aspect at the matte's own width: 256×144 for 16:9. The torch matte is already at the input's aspect.
  - The array is uploaded as it is into the R8 `<name>#mask`, so its rows are top first. `SegmentNode.rows_top_first` is True in person mode and False in motion mode (a GL pass).
  - Every pass that samples a mask honours the flag through `graph.mask_of`:
    - `CUT_FS` (T3);
    - `PROMOTE_FS` and `MaskPasses.promote(flip=)`, used by `MaskPicture`;
    - `MaskPasses.fit(flip=)`, which a new `graph.mask_port` uses. Both `MatteNode.image` and `SegmentNode.image` now go through `mask_port`, so `lookback.cover` gets the matte refitted and turned.
  - The readback path's array is GL's (bottom first). It is handed to the worker as `buf[::-1]`, so the worker always sees rows top first. A model expects an upright person.
- **Other options.**
  - Flip the array on the CPU before uploading, and drop the flag (T3's 4xl option).
  - Resample on the GPU.
  - Stretch-fit Vision's matte in the consumers, which would need a per-mask fit mode.
- **To reverse.** Upload `mask[::-1]` and make `rows_top_first` False. The flag's passes stay harmless.
- **Rough edge.** The matte's own size differs from Vision's documented size. On a frame much wider than 16:9 the width stays 256, so the vertical resolution drops.
- **Judge it.** AJ.1 (the right way up, and on the person rather than beside them).

### 4xu. The shortcut is taken when the wire into `segment.image` comes straight from a channel whose newest frame has CPU pixels and whose fit is cover

- **Answer.** controller, 2026-10-27, inside W4's "when the wire comes straight from a `channel.<c>` whose newest `MediaFrame` has CPU pixels".
- **Built.**
  - **The shortcut.** It is taken when all of these hold:
    - `inputs["image"]` is a `MediaNode` with no `alpha_of`;
    - `Renderer.media_now(c)` returns the frame its texture was uploaded from this frame, together with the channel's fit;
    - `frame.pixels is not None`;
    - the fit is 0 (cover).

    The channel's array is then submitted as it is, with no readback.
  - **The readback.** Anything else goes through `MaskPasses.readback`: a trilinear downsample to `worker.input_size` (Vision: long side 640 / 960 / 1280 by level; torch: short side 240 / 360 / 480), then one `read_into`. That covers a Syphon channel, a channel fitted by contain or stretch, and any picture that is not a channel.
  - **When it submits.** Only when the input moved, and only while the worker has nothing waiting (`worker.wants()`). "Moved" means:
    - for a channel: its `MediaFrame.frame` id or the level changed;
    - for any other picture: every frame.
  - **Counting.** Each path is counted on the node (`SegmentNode.taken`).
- **Other options.**
  - Take the shortcut for every fit, and fit the matte as the channel's fit does. That needs a per-mask fit mode.
  - Read back every frame regardless of `wants()`.
- **To reverse.** One condition in `SegmentNode._feed`.
- **Rough edge.**
  - A camera channel set to contain or stretch pays the readback, about 0.4 ms at the dream's size. That is unmeasured here.
  - A still is segmented once per level and never again. That is the point, but a backend error on that one submit is not retried until the frame or the level moves.
- **Judge it.** AJ.1, AJ.8 (frame time with two cameras).

### 4xv. Motion mode: `smoothstep(sense ± 0.04)` on the largest channel's difference, and `learn` is a time constant in seconds

- **Answer.** controller, 2026-10-27. The spec gives `smoothstep(t − s, t + s, |frame − bg|)` with `sense` as t, but does not name s. Hand check 4 reads "stand still for `segment.learn` seconds".
- **Built.**
  - **The difference.** `MOTION_FS` takes `d = max(|Δr|, |Δg|, |Δb|, |Δa|)` of the premultiplied picture against the background, and the matte is `smoothstep(sense − 0.04, sense + 0.04, d)` (`masks.MOTION_SOFT`).
  - **The learning.** `LEARN_FS` writes `bg ← mix(bg, picture, k)` with `k = 1 − exp(−dt / learn)`, where dt is the frame's clock step, clamped to 0–0.25 s.
  - **The background.** It is a half-float pair (`Renderer.motion_pair(name)`, a `DelayPair`) at the frame's size. The first frame, or a new size, learns the picture whole (k = 1), so nothing is foreground on frame one.
  - **Defaults.** sense 0.1 and learn 3 s.
  - **Where it runs.** On the frame thread, with no worker. It runs in the preview too, which keeps its own background model (row 4xx).
- **Other options.**
  - Luma difference instead of the largest channel.
  - A `segment.soft` param.
  - `learn` as a per-frame rate.
- **To reverse.** One constant, or one shader line.
- **Rough edge.**
  - It keys shadows and the projector's own light on the room (the spike says so).
  - A party light sweeping the room makes everything foreground until it learns.
  - In the `segment-motion` fixture the polytope's animated face pattern counts as motion, so the whole polytope is foreground and not only its moving edges.
- **Judge it.** AJ.4.

### 4xw. A backend error mid-show passes the picture through whole until the next good result

- **Answer.** controller, 2026-10-27, from spec W4 ("errors in words, never a crash") and hand check 9.
- **Built.**
  - **Mid-show errors.** An exception in `backend.run` is caught on the worker's thread into `.error`, and the worker lives on. While `worker.error` is set the node makes no matte: `fg` is the input whole, `bg` and `mask` are nothing, and the node says the error. The next good result clears it.
  - **Startup errors.** A backend that will not open (`open()` raises) gives a permanent reason. A missing library is a reason string from `pick_backend` and starts no thread.
  - **Lost input.** A channel that loses its feed reads nothing, so the node says `no frame on channel a: its picture through, whole`. When the feed comes back, the frame id moves and it resumes.
- **Other options.** Keep showing the last good matte through an error, which is stale and could go wrong on a moving dancer.
- **To reverse.** One branch in `SegmentNode._make`.
- **Rough edge.** A flaky backend that fails every other frame flickers between cut and whole.
- **Judge it.** AJ.9.

### 4xx. The worker exists on the program renderer only; the preview's segment passes through, and its motion runs on its own model

- **Answer.** controller, 2026-10-27, from B3's review seam with plan 38.
- **Built.**
  - **Who makes a worker.** `Renderer.segment_cfg` defaults to None. `segmenter(name)` returns None for such a renderer, and app.py sets `segment_cfg` on the program renderer only. A person-mode segment with no worker says `no worker in the preview: its picture through, whole`.
  - **Motion in the preview.** Motion mode is GLSL only, so it runs in the preview. The preview's `motion_pair` is its own: it learns its own background from the same frames, so it matches the program's within a frame or two.
  - **Closing.** `Renderer.close()` closes this renderer's own workers only. They live in its `_segmenters` dict, and nothing is shared. A test mirrors a second renderer with no `segment_cfg`: it builds the same graph, makes no worker, and closing it leaves the program's worker alive.
  - **Workers kept or closed.** A worker is kept by node name across rebuilds. It is closed `SIZED_KEEP_FRAMES` (30) frames after no graph holds its node (`_tend_segments`, called in `_draw_frame` beside `_idle_rings`), and again in `Renderer.close`, which app.py's close list calls at quit and restart.
  - **GL on close.** `Renderer.close` releases no GL, because at that point of the exit the context may not be current. The motion pairs go with the context.
- **Other options.**
  - Share the program's worker results with the preview, which needs a hand-over plan 38 does not have.
  - Give the preview no motion either.
- **To reverse.** Have `hold.mirror` hand the preview the program's `segment_cfg`. Workers would then double.
- **Rough edge.** The preview shows the input whole where the program shows a cut person, so a held program and its preview differ by design.
- **Judge it.** AI/AJ cross-check: hold a preset with a segment and look at both.

### 4xy. The torch backend's weights live in `<library home>/weights`, then torch's hub cache, else they are downloaded

- **Answer.** controller, 2026-10-27, from spec W4 ("like the dream's").
- **Built.**
  - **Where it looks.** `segment.find_weights` looks for `lraspp_mobilenet_v3_large-d234d4ea.pth` (13 MB) in `[segment] weights_dir`, which defaults to `library.home()/weights`, then in `torch.hub.get_dir()/checkpoints`.
  - **The download.** Otherwise it downloads into the first, after the line `segment: downloading … to …` on stderr.
  - **The model.** It is built with `weights=None, weights_backbone=None` and the state loaded, so no ImageNet backbone is fetched. It is fp16 on MPS when that is available, else fp32 on CPU. `[segment] device` can override the device.
  - **Sharing.** One model per (device, dtype) is shared by every worker, with its forward pass serialised by a lock.
- **Other options.** Ship the weights in the repo.
- **To reverse.** Change one path function.
- **Rough edge.** The torch backend shares MPS with the dream and the renderer (spike risk). `auto` picks Vision on macOS, so torch only runs when asked for.
- **Judge it.** None (a config setting at the machine).

## 3. As-built notes (T4)

**Built, in the plan's terms.**

- **`segment.py` (new).**
  - `SegmentWorker` follows `DreamWorker`'s shape: three rotating input slots that the submit copies into, one pending job, latest wins with drops counted, a lock-free `latest() -> (mask, index, (w, h))`, errors kept in `.error`, a thread named `segment-<name>` that sleeps on a condition, and `close()` that joins within `CLOSE_S` = 1 s.
  - The backends `VisionBackend` and `TorchBackend` have `open()`, `run(frame, quality)`, `close()` and `input_size()`.
  - `pick_backend(cfg)` returns a backend or a reason string. It finds libraries without importing them.
  - `make_worker(cfg, name)` builds a worker.
  - The module imports numpy and the standard library at its top. Vision, Quartz, Foundation, torch and torchvision are imported inside `open()` and `run()` only.
- **`graph.py`.**
  - Tables: `SegmentAt` and its reader, plus every table line the matte and the cut have. The pins `segment.fg` / `.bg` / `.mask` are in `PINS` for instance 1, and `segment.N.*` is matched by `_SEGMENT_PIN` through `pin_node` and `segment_pin`.
  - Pin kinds are `fg` and `bg` (IMAGE) and `mask` (MASK). `WIRED_MAKES["segment"]` is None, so the bare node is no source, and it is refused in its pins' words. The scene-pin refusal is narrowed to the scene's pins.
  - `SegmentNode` (KIND "segment", OUTPUT MASK) is the matte. `SegmentPin` is fg / bg, a cut over it.
  - `mask_port` is new, and both `MatteNode.image` and `SegmentNode.image` use it. `MaskPicture.paint` reads `mask_of` and passes the flip.
  - `Frame.segments` is new.
- **`masks.py`.**
  - `PROMOTE_FS` takes `u_flip`, and `promote` and `fit` take `flip=`. `fit` no longer returns the mask itself when it must be turned.
  - New: `MOTION_FS`, `LEARN_FS`, `DOWN_FS`, `MOTION_SOFT`, and the methods `feather`, `motion`, `learn` and `readback` (a trilinear sampler kept and released with the passes).
- **`render.py`.**
  - `segment_cfg` (None by default), `_segmenters` and `_motions`.
  - `segmenter(name)`: an optional `segment_factory(cfg, name)` hook for tests, else `segment.make_worker`, imported lazily.
  - `motion_pair(name)`, `_tend_segments(graph)` (called in `_draw_frame` beside `_idle_rings`) and `close()`.
  - `media_now(c) -> (MediaFrame | None, fit)`: `media_texture` now keeps the frame it read under `("frame", c)`.
- **`app.py`.** `renderer.segment_cfg = cfg.get("segment", {})` and `segmenters = _Closer(renderer.close)` beside the dream's. `segmenters` is added to both `close_all` lists (quit and restart). Nothing is near `_draw_render`.
- **`params.py` / `view.py` / `preset.py`.**
  - `declare_segments`, `segment_ids`, `is_segment_id` and `SEGMENT_GROUP`; `segment` after `cut` in `PERFORMANCE_GROUPS`; `build_registry` binds `ViewState.segments`.
  - In `view.py`: `SegmentState`, `SEGMENT_MODES`, `SEGMENT_QUALITY`, `SEGMENT_FEATHER` and `SEGMENT_LEARN`.
  - The landing is beside the cuts. A load defers `segment.*` values, a new preset leaves them out, and the group is in `NO_MODULE`. `ui_presets.OVERVIEW_GROUPS` excludes `segment` (one word, as T2 and T3 did).
- **`graphview.py`.** Table entries only: `OWNERS`, `LABELS`, `HOVERS`, `FRAME_CLASS` and `PICKER_GROUP` ("pictures", with the channels), plus `_node_name`.
- **`pyproject.toml` / `uv.lock`.** `pyobjc-framework-vision>=12.2 ; sys_platform == 'darwin'`, which pulls in `pyobjc-framework-coreml`.
- **Tests.**
  - `tests/test_segment.py`: 10 tests, 2 of them live.
  - `tests/test_graph_segment.py`: 23 tests.
  - Changed for this task:
    - `test_app_boundary.py`: the gated list gains `hyperspace.segment`, `Vision`, `Vision._Vision`, `CoreML` and `torchvision`.
    - `test_graph.py`: the banned imports gain `segment` and `Vision`.
    - `test_hotreload.py`: `segment` is added to `cold`.
    - `test_dream.py`: the torch-import check reads `segment.py`'s top-level AST.
    - `test_golden_frames.py`: count +8, `GOLDENS_SINCE`, `no_hands`.
    - `tools/golden_frames.py`: `MEDIA_FIXTURES` and `WIRED_FIXTURES`.
- **Fixture.** `tests/fixtures/presets/segment-motion.json` (`/7`) has 8 goldens and one new PNG. It was recorded with `--only segment-motion` and `HYPERSPACE_HOME` set to a scratch folder with an empty `config.toml`. The golden count is 781 → 789, and none moved.

**Differs from the brief or spec, and why.**

## 5. Review round 1 additions

### 4yb. `fg` and `bg` are cut at the frame's size; the input is read through `OWN_FIT`

- **Answer.** controller with the reviewer, 2026-10-27 (T4 review). W4 says "fg and bg at the input's size". A segment pin is a frame-sized picture: it reads its input as `picture(frame, src)` (an own-sized input fitted by `OWN_FIT`) and cuts it at the frame's size.
- **Built.** `SegmentPin.made` makes `<name>#cut` at `frame.size`, or at a sized frame's size (for example the faces at `FACE_PIC`). `SegmentPin` has no `own_size`, so a consumer of another size gets the picture as an effect's would be. The matte is fitted into it by cover.
- **Other options.** Make `fg`/`bg` own-sized as T3's `cut` is: cut at the input's own size, then refit, keeping its resolution and letting own-sizes chain downstream.
- **To reverse.** Port `CutNode`'s `own_size`, `_at_own` and `#fit` path into `SegmentPin`.
- **Rough edge.** An own-sized input (a resize at 64², a delay) loses its own resolution and own-size chaining past the segment. The placement is consistent with every other frame-sized picture.
- **Judge it.** None (no hand check needs an own-sized input).

### 4yc. The smoothed matte is kept in float; `smooth` is held under 0.99

- **Answer.** controller with the reviewer, 2026-10-27 (T4 review, Important 1).
- **Built.** `SegmentNode._prev` is float32. Each result is mixed as `prev·k + new·(1−k)`, and the upload is `round(prev)` as uint8. A region that leaves at k = 0.99 decays to 0 in about 620 results instead of stalling at about 50/255, which was a permanent ghost of about 20%. `k` is clamped to at most 0.99, because 1 would never move.
- **Other options.** Keep the uint8 path and nudge each step by at least one unit towards the new value.
- **To reverse.** One block in `_pull`.
- **Rough edge.** At 0.99 a full fade takes about 20 s at 30 results a second. The range is the param's; the clamp only stops it freezing.
- **Judge it.** AJ.3.

### 4yd. A segment's `image` is a picture path for laid-over-ness when its `fg` or `bg` is laid over

- **Answer.** controller with the reviewer, 2026-10-27 (T4 review, Important 2). This is the rule T3's cut was held to (row 4xo).
- **Built.** `_Build.laid_over` recurses through `kind == "segment" and w.port == "image"` when `<segment>.fg` or `<segment>.bg` is laid over. It works for `segment.N` too. With `scene → segment.image` and `segment.fg → composite.over`, the scene clears see-through (`Scene.ground` 0). So before the first matte, off, in the preview, or on an error, `fg` covers the under only where the scene draws. The mask pin is not a picture path.
- **Other options.** None. Without this, inserting a segment changes the picture where nothing is cut.
- **To reverse.** Drop the clause.
- **Rough edge.** As with an effect or a cut: one node, one ground. A scene also wired elsewhere is see-through there too.
- **Judge it.** AJ.1 (with `segment.on` off: the scene over the under unchanged).
