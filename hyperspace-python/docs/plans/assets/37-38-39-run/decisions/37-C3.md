# 37-C3 decisions — the backward wire (plan 37 P3, D5 = the rules)

## 1. Register-row draft

### 4xx-a. An effect is laid in the leftmost area of its consumers that is not left of what feeds it

- **Answer.** controller — 2026-10-27. D5's recommendation ("a node whose only consumers are in one area is laid in that area") taken and extended. `acid-scene`'s acid.2 has consumers in **two** areas: the look-back's `image` and `occluder`, and a composite's `under` in EFFECTS. So the rule as written, and the dispatch's tie-break "a node with consumers in several areas keeps today's area", would have left the backward wire (`wire:acid.2>lookback/occluder`) in place. The built rule reduces to D5 only when the one consumer area is **left of EFFECTS**. An effect whose consumers all sit to its right (OUTPUTS, the usual case) never moves; neither does one whose consumers are all in EFFECTS. So D5's "laid in that area" is not applied rightwards.
- **Built.** `graphview.laid_areas(view)`, called in `graphview.build` where every node gets its area. An effect, copy, composite or resize (a frame node `area_of` puts in EFFECTS) whose consumers sit left of the effects is laid in the leftmost of their areas that is not left of what feeds it. On GRAPH, `acid-scene`'s acid.2 now stands in LOOK-BACK, in front of the look-back, and every cable from it runs right. `area_of` (the table's answer) is unchanged. Tie-breaks:
  1. Consumers in several areas: the leftmost one that the node's feeds allow.
  2. A consumer of no area (a hand-made view's node) is not counted.
  3. A cable kept for the next frame (`kept`: into a look-back's or a delay's `image`, or an old D1 `after X`) does not count. So a post-look-back chain that records into the look-back does not all slide into LOOK-BACK.
  4. A consumer left of what feeds the node (a scene picture taken back into a switch with the media) is a return by design and does not count.
  5. A chain whose only consumer is in another area moves whole (consumers first, to a fixed point).
  6. Only EFFECTS-area frame nodes move. The scene, the look-back, the media, the modulation and the controllers keep their table areas: an LFO feeding only scene params is never moved, nor is a look-back taken back into the faces.
- **Other options.** D5 read literally (one consumer area only), which does not fix `acid-scene`. Re-lay `acid-scene` by hand (it is a fixture, not a factory preset, and it carries no positions). Move every node kind by the rule (this would carry LFOs into the scene and the scene into the media).
- **To reverse.** Drop the three-line loop in `build` that applies `laid_areas`, and its tests (`test_no_cable_runs_backwards_across_areas[fixture:acid-scene]`, `test_a_node_is_laid_where_its_consumers_are`). Restoring D5 literally instead means restricting `laid_areas` to nodes whose counted consumers share one area. Cost if wrong: one cable drawn backwards on one fixture.
- **Rough edge.** An effect that moves takes its area's chips and title framing with it: the EFFECTS chip no longer frames acid.2, LOOK-BACK's does. The rule applies to a placed node's *area* too (what the chips frame, the depths of its area-mates), never to its *position*: `layout` skips every saved position. A moved effect does not wrap in `SHADER_ROW` rows (only EFFECTS wraps).
- **Judge it.** Check AH.7.

## 2. Hand-check draft

**AH.7. `acid-scene` on GRAPH: no cable runs backwards.** (laptop, a scratch home with no `[library]` and no devices)
Headless: `tests/test_graph_areas.py::test_no_cable_runs_backwards_across_areas` (every factory preset and every graph fixture), `::test_a_node_is_laid_where_its_consumers_are`, `::test_auto_layout_stacks_each_area_and_keeps_saved_positions` (no layout runs by itself).
1. Copy `tests/fixtures/presets/acid-scene.json` into the scratch home's `presets/`.
2. Load it and open GRAPH. If anything was saved, press **tidy** once.
3. Follow acid.2: from the scene, into the look-back's picture and its occluder, and on to the composite.
4. Drag acid.2 somewhere else, wait a few frames, then reload.

5. (The rough edge.) In a scratch copy of a user preset with a saved position for an acid.2-style effect (one that sits in the EFFECTS region and feeds the look-back), click the LOOK-BACK chip or title.

*Pass:* acid.2 stands in LOOK-BACK, left of the look-back. Every cable out of it runs left to right, and the EFFECTS area starts with the composite. After the drag it stays where dropped, through later frames and after the reload. In step 5, expect a known rough edge, not a fail: the saved effect keeps its position in the EFFECTS region, but its area is now LOOK-BACK. So the LOOK-BACK chip and title frame reach over to that node, and the EFFECTS frame does not claim it. Judge whether this reads acceptably on a Drive preset saved before C3. A **tidy** lays the effect into LOOK-BACK and ends it.
*Fail:* acid.2 is in EFFECTS with a cable curling back into the look-back (the rule is not applied: `laid_areas` is not called in `build`). Or a node jumps back after a drag (a layout running by itself).

## 3. As-built notes (C3)

- **Built.**
  - `graphview.laid_areas` and `graphview.area_column` (an area's place left to right, `OTHER` at the audio's).
  - `graphview.kept`, factored out of `returns` with no change in behaviour.
  - The call in `build`, after every node's `area_of`.
  - Tests:
    - A parametrised sweep: every factory preset (25) loaded as the preset, and every graph fixture (22) loaded as its own preset. It asserts that no cable whose ends sit in different areas runs from an area into one that is not right of it, less `returns`' loops by design.
    - A test of the rule's cases: acid-scene; an effect fed by nothing into the faces → SCENE; a chain of two into the look-back's occluder → both in LOOK-BACK; an effect fed by the look-back and kept into it → stays in EFFECTS; a saved position wins.
    - N9's own sweep (`test_every_node_has_one_area_and_the_three_concerns_are_separate`) tightened: its acid-scene exemption is gone (`back_wires == []`).
- **What failed before C3.** Exactly one case: `fixture:acid-scene`, with `wire:acid.2>lookback/occluder` running EFFECTS → LOOK-BACK. `wire:acid.2>lookback/image` ran back too, but it is a kept wire and already a return by design. Every factory preset already passed. `echo-faces`' `lookback → shape.faces` is a taken-back return by design and is exempt, as `returns` says.
- **No layout runs by itself.** The test that keeps this green is `tests/test_graph_areas.py::test_auto_layout_stacks_each_area_and_keeps_saved_positions` (its tail, "2026-10-26: no layout runs on its own; only tidy lays out again"). It is green.
- **Differs from the dispatch.**
  - The dispatch calls `acid-scene` "the factory preset (`/7`)" under `hyperspace/library/presets/`. There is no such preset: `acid-scene` exists only as the test fixture `tests/fixtures/presets/acid-scene.json` (`/2`, loaded through the migration).
  - The dispatch guessed that the effect feeds `shape.faces`. It feeds the look-back and a composite.
  - The dispatch's tie-break "a node with consumers in several areas keeps today's area" would not fix the wire. The leftmost-consumer extension (row 4xx-a) does, and it equals D5 when there is one consumer area.
  - "Only for nodes with no saved position": the rule names the area in `build`, which knows no positions. It never moves a placed node, because `layout` skips every saved position (asserted in the new test and in the test named above). A placed effect's *area* (chips, framing) does follow the rule.
- **Step 3 (saved positions forcing the wire).** Moot. No factory preset carries a `layout` key, `acid-scene` carries none either, and of the fixtures only `glimmer-bug` has one (it passes). No preset edited.
- **Not verified by a person.** The look on the canvas (AH.7), and whether the owner accepts this reading of D5.
- **Rough edges.** See row 4xx-a. `ruff check .` reports 25 errors on the base, none of them in the files touched: they are in the `docs/plans/assets/39-vision` benches, `hyperspace/media/*`, `tools/ingest.py` and three tests, and are not C3's.
