## 1. Register-row drafts (numbered 4xx; the docs task places them in 440–459)

### 4xk. `cut.amount` scales the cut-away part, colour and alpha together: `picture × (1 − amount)`

- **Answer.** controller, 2026-10-27. W3 says "1 cuts to nothing, 0.5 dims the cut part", and the brief gives `c = src × mix(1, m, amount)`.
- **Built.** `CUT_FS` writes `src × mix(1, m, amount)`, with `m` the mask (inverted on `cut.invert`). Where the mask fails (`m` = 0), the pixel is `src × (1 − amount)`. The picture is premultiplied, so rgb and alpha scale together.
  - At 0.5 the cut part is half as bright and half as opaque. On a `composite.over` that is a 50/50 mix with what lies under.
  - At 0 the picture is whole.
  - Where the mask passes, nothing changes.
  - Default 1; range 0–1, modulatable.
- **Other options.** Dim the colour only and keep alpha, so a dimmed opaque area covers what is under. Treat `amount` as a threshold on the mask instead.
- **To reverse.** One line of `CUT_FS`. Saved values keep their number.
- **Rough edge.** "Dims" on an opaque output (a cut straight into a window) reads as darker. Over a composite it reads as half see-through. Both follow from premultiplied alpha.
- **Judge it.** AJ.5 (turn `cut.amount` from 1 to 0.5 by eye).

### 4xl. The flip flag is a method on the mask maker, `rows_top_first(frame) -> bool`, read through `graph.mask_of`; `CUT_FS` honours it with `u_flip`

- **Answer.** controller, 2026-10-27 (spec "Risks": orientation; the brief: "T4 sets the flag; you define it").
- **Built.**
  - `graph.mask_of(node, frame) -> (texture, flipped)` returns the maker's own-size mask (`native`) when it has one, else what a mask port binds (`image`).
  - `flipped` is `node.rows_top_first(frame)` when the maker defines that method, else False.
  - The method is per frame, so a segment node can say True for a Vision or torch matte (a CPU array uploaded as it is) and False for its GL motion matte.
  - `MaskPasses.cut(…, flip=)` sets `u_flip`, which reads the mask at `1 − v`.
  - Every GL-made mask (matte, cover) has its first row at the bottom, as every texture here does, and is never flipped.
- **Other options.**
  - A class attribute: not enough, because segment's two modes differ.
  - Flip the array on the CPU before upload: one copy per result, and then no flag at all. T4 may still choose this, and `mask_of` keeps working.
  - Keep the flag on the texture object.
- **To reverse.** Drop the method and the uniform, and have T4 upload flipped arrays.
- **Rough edge.** Only the cut honours the flag. `PROMOTE_FS` (a mask read as a picture) and `MaskPasses.fit` (a mask port's refit) do not yet. A rows-top-first `segment.mask` wired into an output or into `lookback.cover` would show upside down until T4 adds `u_flip` there and makes `MaskPicture`/`fit` read `mask_of`.
- **Judge it.** AJ.1 (`segment.fg`/`.bg`, T4's), AJ.5.

### 4xm. A cut makes its picture once per frame into a target of its own, then shows or refits it; it never cuts straight into the consumer's framebuffer

- **Answer.** controller, 2026-10-27 (inside W3).
- **Built.** `CutNode.made` runs the one pass into an RGBA8 frame target `<name>#cut`, at the frame's key (`(<name>#cut, size)` at its own size).
  - `image` returns that target. An own-sized cut is refitted once per frame into a half-float `<name>#fit` at the frame's size, as an effect's is.
  - `paint` shows the target 1:1, or lays it in by cover from its own size.
  - This matches how resize and matte work, and the pass never reads the framebuffer it writes, whichever pool texture the consumer hands in.
- **Other options.** Cut straight into `dst` when the sizes match. That saves one full-frame copy, at the risk of reading a pool texture while writing it.
- **To reverse.** A direct path in `paint` for the frame-sized case.
- **Rough edge.** One extra RGBA8 frame-sized target and one copy per cut per frame.
- **Judge it.** None (the SYSTEM page's `cut` line).

### 4xn. With no mask, or a mask making nothing, a cut is its picture through, byte-identical; fed no picture, it is dormant

- **Answer.** controller, 2026-10-27. W2 says "a cut fed by it shows its picture whole"; W3 says "no mask wired: the picture through, untouched".
- **Built.**
  - With no mask, `image` and `paint` are the input's own: no pass, no `#cut` target. The test compares the output's bytes with the picture wired straight.
  - A matte reading nothing (an empty channel) counts the same.
  - The node says one of:
    - "no mask wired: its picture through, whole"
    - "matte makes nothing: its picture through, whole"
    - "cut by matte" (with "· inverted" and/or "· amount 0.50" when set)
  - When nothing is wired into `cut.image`, the cut is not built: "cut is fed by nothing here: wire its image".
- **Other options.** With no mask, cut everything (the picture vanishes) or keep nothing.
- **To reverse.** One branch in `CutNode.cuts`.
- **Rough edge.** None.
- **Judge it.** AJ.5.

### 4xo. A cut passes laid-over-ness through, as an effect does: where the mask passes, the picture is what it would be without the cut

- **Answer.** controller with the reviewer, 2026-10-27 (T3 review). Inserting a cut must not change the picture inside the area the mask passes. That is the cut's own contract, and it matters for the owner's `fg → cut → composite.over` case.
- **Built.** `_Build.laid_over` recurses through `kind in ("effect", "cut") and w.port == "image"`. The `mask` port is not a picture path.
  - `scene → kaleido → cut.image`, `cut → composite.over` lays the scene over: `Scene.ground` is 0, the kaleido is `laid_over`, and `CUT_FS` multiplies the see-through alpha it receives.
  - Inside the disc in `cut-out`, the scene under it shows through the kaleidoscope's empty space.
  - A cut straight into an output lays nothing over (`ground` None).
  - The `cut-out` goldens were re-recorded in the same task (commit 302b5cd); none of the other 773 moved.
- **Other options.** Leave `laid_over` following effects only. The scene then keeps its opaque ground inside the passing area, so inserting a cut changes the picture there.
- **To reverse.** Drop `"cut"` from that tuple, and re-record `cut-out`.
- **Rough edge.**
  - A scene that is also wired elsewhere (as in `cut-out`, where it is the under too) is see-through everywhere it is drawn. This is the effect path's rule today: one node, one ground. The zero ground is premultiplied transparent black, the same rgb.
  - `laid_over` still does not see through `copy`, `resize` or `delay`. That is a rough edge for the docs task, not built here.
- **Judge it.** AJ.5.

### 4xp. `cut` is its own performance group, left out of the module overview, as matte and resize are

- **To reverse.** One word in each tuple.
- **Rough edge.** None.
- **Judge it.** None.

### 4xq. `cut-out` is a wired fixture: goldens yes, no frame-signature case

## 3. As-built notes (T3)

**Built, in the plan's terms.**

- **`cut[.n]` (W3).**
  - `CutAt` and its reader in `NODE_KINDS`, `RESERVED` (only the word itself: an effect file named `cutter` is still an effect), `DECLARED`, `_wired_kind` (`_CUT`), `WIRED_MAKES` (IMAGE), `WIRED_TAKES` (`{"image": IMAGE, "mask": MASK}`), the from/to words, `_WireCtx.cuts`, `GraphSpec.cuts`, `read_graph`'s context, and the `_Build._make` branch.
  - A picture into `cut.mask` is refused with T2's words ("… put a matte between"). A cut is a picture everywhere a picture is taken (tested: outputs, composite over/under, an effect, the faces, another matte).
- **`CutNode`.**
  - Inputs `image` (IMAGE) and `mask` (MASK); output IMAGE; timed.
  - The mask port gets the mask itself, never its promoted picture. `_taken` only promotes into IMAGE ports.
  - Its own size is its picture's (`own_size` passes through). An own-sized picture is cut at that size (`Frame.at`), with the mask fitted into it by cover. A consumer of another size gets it by cover (`OWN_FIT`), as an effect's picture.
  - It cuts only when its picture reads and its mask makes a texture (`cuts`). Otherwise it is its picture through (row 4xn).
  - The pass is `masks.MaskPasses.cut` (T2's `CUT_FS` with T2's cover `u_scale`, plus `u_flip`). It is made once per frame into its own target (row 4xm).
  - `said` as in row 4xn.
- **`masks.py`.** `CUT_FS` gains `u_flip`, and `MaskPasses.cut` gains `flip=`. Its docstring names T4's `segment.fg`/`.bg` as the other callers: `cut(src, mask, dst, invert=…, amount=1, flip=…)` is the function T4 calls with a mask texture and an invert flag.
- **`graph.mask_of`.** The flag's reader (row 4xl). It is a module function, so T4's `MaskPicture`/`fit` can adopt it.
- **Params.**
  - `view.CutState` (`invert` False, `amount` 1.0) and `ViewState.cuts`.
  - `params.declare_cuts` on `declare_mattes`' pattern: ids `cut[.n].invert` (toggle) and `cut[.n].amount` (0–1), group `cut`, modulatable.
  - Also `cut_ids`, `is_cut_id` and `CUT_GROUP`; `build_registry` binds `ViewState.cuts`; `Frame.cuts` reads the view state.
- **Preset.** Declared on land beside the mattes. A load defers `cut.*` values, a new preset leaves them out, and `NO_MODULE` gains the group. The `/7` round trip of `{"kind": "cut"}` and its values is tested through two rigs. The schema is unchanged.
- **GRAPH.** Table entries only: `OWNERS` (the resize's rule), `LABELS` ("Cut"), `HOVERS`, `FRAME_CLASS`, `PICKER_GROUP` (`masks`), `_node_name`. No picker item and no `render_add` branch: T5's.
- **Fixture.** `tests/fixtures/presets/cut-out.json` (`/7`, wired):
  - channel a is `backdrop-still.png`;
  - `matte` (luma, key 0.95, feather 4) → `cut.mask`;
  - `scene → kaleido` (zoom 0.5, so a wedge holds more of the polytope and the disc reads by eye) → `cut.image`;
  - `cut → composite.over`, `scene → composite.under`, `composite → output.all.image`;
  - `shape → scene.geometry`, `pattern → shape.faces`.

  It has 8 goldens: 4 scene states share one picture, and 4 hands/none states are the black frame. The golden count goes from 773 to 781, and none moved.
- **Tests.** `tests/test_graph_cut.py`, 23 tests: identity and tables; entry round trip; must be declared; the type rule both ways; the build (the mask itself, dormant without a picture, mask None when unwired); `mask_of`'s flag; the canvas tables; `declare_cuts`; registry → `ViewState`; the fixture's `/7` round trip; a new preset holds no `cut.*`. In GL they cover:
  - the hole is (0,0,0,0) in the cut's own target;
  - the under shows through it on a composite;
  - amount 0.5 halves colour and alpha (and gives a 50/50 mix on the composite);
  - invert;
  - amount 0 leaves the picture whole;
  - no mask is byte-identical to the picture wired straight;
  - a matte reading nothing is byte-identical too;
  - orientation: an asymmetric still's mask keeps the top-left only;
  - the flip: a rows-top-first CPU mask read the right way up only with the flag;
  - an own-sized (resize 64×36) picture is cut at 64×36 and laid into 160×90 by cover.

**Differs from the brief or spec, and why.**
- No frame-signature case (row 4xq).
- `view.py` gained `CutState` and `ViewState.cuts`. `view.py` is not in the brief's boundary list, but it is where T2 put `MatteState`, and `graph.py` and `params.py` both import their states from it.
- `ui_presets.py` got a one-word change (row 4xp).
- The flip flag is honoured by the cut alone (row 4xl).

**Not verified by any person.** Every picture comes from a test or a golden. Nobody has looked at a cut at the machine. The cost of one cut (the pass plus one copy) is unmeasured on the show Mac.

**Known rough edges.**
- A cut cannot be added from the GRAPH picker until T5.
- A scene laid over through a cut is see-through everywhere it is drawn, as through an effect (row 4xo).
- `laid_over` does not see through `copy`, `resize` or `delay` (docs task).
- The flip flag in `PROMOTE_FS`/`fit` is T4's (the controller puts it in T4's dispatch).
- Only the cut honours the flip flag (row 4xl).
- There is one extra frame-sized copy per cut (row 4xm).
