### 4tb. The cut-out starter: a new segment and a new composite each time, never refused, taking All outputs' picture and saying what fed it

- **Answer.** controller — 2026-10-27. W6 gives the four wires. The brief says "refusing nothing" and "`STARTER_SAID` names what it replaced".
- **Built.** `STARTERS` gains `("cut-out", "cut-out")`. It lands in one step (`starter: cut-out`):
  - a new `segment[.n]` and a new `composite[.n]` (blend over);
  - `channel.a → <seg>.image`, `<seg>.fg → <comp>.over`, `scene → <comp>.under`, `<comp> → output.all.image`, in place of whatever fed `output.all.image`.

  Placement: the new nodes are laid left to right from where you right-clicked (`edit.placed`), with the scene first when the section had none, then the segment, then the composite. Channel a and All outputs stay where they are. A second cut-out makes `segment.2` and `composite.2` and takes All outputs' picture from the first composite.

  Its words: "cut-out: channel.a → segment.image, segment.fg over the scene in composite → All outputs". This gains " — in place of X into All outputs" when something else fed it, and " — left unwired: scene.geometry (wire a shape in: the scene draws nothing)" when no shape is wired into the scene. The starter does not add a shape, because W6 lists four wires and the WYSIWYG rule forbids filling anything in.
- **Other options.**
  - Reuse an existing segment or composite.
  - Refuse when All outputs is already fed.
  - Add `shape → scene.geometry` on a blank preset, as the scene chain does.
  - Not offer it from a cable's menu (it is not offered there today: `CABLE_STARTERS` is unchanged).
- **To reverse.** One branch in `render_starter` and one `STARTERS` entry.
- **Rough edge.**
  - On a blank preset the scene is empty until a shape is wired in. The words say so.
  - Channel a is fixed. On a rig whose camera is on another channel, the wire must be moved by hand.
- **Judge it.** AJ.1.

### 4td. A cable is MASK-typed (purple) when its maker makes a mask, whatever port takes it

- **Answer.** controller — 2026-10-27. W6 says "a promoted mask cable is drawn purple into a red port". The T2 review located the fix.
- **Built.** In `graphview.build`, a section wire's `VCable.type` is `MASK` when its source pin is a mask (`matte`, `segment[.n].mask`, `scene.cover`). Otherwise it is the destination port's type, as before. `ui_graph.COLOURS` is unchanged: it already colours MASK purple, and the port's own square stays red.
- **Other options.** Add a separate colour field on `VCable` and change `ui_graph._cable`, which is a plan 37/38 file.
- **To reverse.** One line.
- **Rough edge.** A mask cable is no longer a picture cable, so a promoted mask cable into a picture port has no "insert effect ▸" or "starters ▸" in its menu, and dropping an effect onto it does not place the effect. Two things would have to change to allow those three actions:
  - `ui_graph`'s `c.type == IMAGE` checks on the cable menu and on drop-onto-cable (a plan 37/38 file);
  - `graphview._cable_ends` (with `_picture_port`), which refused these actions on a promoted mask cable even before T5, because the cable's source makes a mask.

  This is a row for the docs task: a later pass, not T5. Until then, to put an effect on a promoted mask, wire the matte into the effect's `image` by hand. *(Review fix.)* The backward-wire layout rule (`returns`) now accepts `c.type in (IMAGE, MASK)`, so a promoted mask into a switch, the backdrop or the pattern sitting left of it runs back as a picture does (`test_a_promoted_mask_into_a_switch_left_of_it_runs_back_by_design`).

### 4tf. A matte, cut or segment the renderer built none of says its state in words

- **Answer.** controller — 2026-10-27. The brief says "words only, no drawing change". A built node already sets its own `said` (T2–T4). Only an unbuilt one (a stand-in) had nothing to say.
- **Built.** `_stand_in` sets `said` for the three kinds from the section's wires into it:
  - matte: "nothing wired into its image: no mask", or "a mask of channel.a's picture";

  - segment: "nothing wired into its image: no worker runs", or "channel.c into it: no worker runs until it is reached".

  `summary` reads these through its existing `said` hook (unchanged, plan 37's), under "reaches nothing".
- **Other options.** The brief's wording "through, untouched". The repo's word everywhere (`CutNode.run`, `SegmentNode`) is "whole", so the stand-in's words match the built node's.
- **To reverse.** Drop the `STAND_IN_SAID` call.
- **Rough edge.** Each source is named by its wire id (`channel.a`), not by the channel's slot name.
