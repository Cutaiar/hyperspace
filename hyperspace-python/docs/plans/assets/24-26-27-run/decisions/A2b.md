## 1. Register-row drafts

**1xx — Strokes in world space by default; screen space is the switch (`view.screen_strokes`)**
- **Built:** with `view.screen_strokes` off (the default) the edges' width and the dots' radius are × `camera.zoom` again, the pre-A2 formula and nothing else: the width is × zoom before the 1 px floor (the sub-pixel part folded into the edge's alpha, so a zoomed-out field's far edges fade as they did on `main`), the radius × zoom before A2's whole-pixel rounding of the diameter. The edges and the dots grow and shrink with the shape as you zoom — the zoomed-out factory looks (ember-orbit 0.5, hex-engine 0.4, party-field 0.42) are back to their pre-A2 dot size and edge weight. With it on, the strokes are A2's: the same pixel weight whatever the zoom (byte-identical to the integration frames before A2b). At `camera.zoom = 1` the two are the same frame byte for byte. One line in `draw_scene` (`sz = 1 if screen_strokes else zoom`), used by the width and the radius. **The shape's strokes only:** the hands are unaffected in both modes — A3's tubes are world-sized geometry (`ghost.py` `WIRE_R`, `BONE_R`) that by design never follows `camera.zoom` (the zoom is the shape's scale, the hands stay with the performer), and A4's "hands as lines" draws the hand triangles' edges at one pixel through `ghost.Geometry.draw(lines=True)`, not through `draw_scene`'s width, so hand lines stay 1 px whichever way the switch is.
- **Other options:** screen space as the default (A2's goldens kept; the zoomed-out looks stay 2–2.5× heavier than on `main` — the rough edge the owner looked at and did not want as the default); **true perspective width** (a stroke a world size, projected per vertex in the shader: near ends thicker than far ends, depth-correct thinning) — not built: it moves every frame with strokes, zoom 1 included, and is a shader change, not a switch on today's arithmetic; a continuous blend between the two (a 0–1 "stroke scaling" mix) — more than the owner asked for.
- **To reverse:** one default (`ViewState.screen_strokes`) and the 218 frames re-recorded back; or the switch removed (one `sz` line, one param, one checkbox).
- **Rough edge:** A2's goldens for strokes at zoom ≠ 1 moved again (218 of 556: all scene-showing states of the 15 factory presets at zoom ≠ 1 with lines or points on, plus hands-orbit with the hands present; and, after A4/A4b merged, the fixture hands-lines' 4 scene frames (zoom 0.9) and party-faces' 2 cloud frames (zoom 1.1 — the shape's edges among the cloud's points; the cloud's points never move with the switch) — see the PR). The pre-A2 behaviour comes back with it: at a big zoom (party-tumble 1.3, party-acid 1.1) edges are fatter, and zoomed far out (party-field 0.42) the far edges fold into alpha and the field looks fainter. A preset saved before A2b has no key and loads world space. At `camera.zoom = 1` the switch shows nothing (both modes draw the same frame) — not a learn "cannot show" rule, since a routed zoom (hands-orbit, party-trip's LFO) makes it live; a desk control on it in a zoom-1 preset with no zoom route moves nothing visible. **This row supersedes the sentence "strokes and dots keep their pixel weight" in A2's row "camera.zoom is the shape's world scale; strokes and dots keep their pixel weight; copies live in the world"**: that is now true only with `view.screen_strokes` on; the row's world scale and copies-in-the-world parts stand.
- **Judge it:** V.A2b.1.

**1xx — The switch's form: `view.screen_strokes`, a boolean, a performance param, routable and modulatable**
- **Built:** `view.screen_strokes` ("Screen strokes"), a toggle in group `view` (performance: in every saved preset's `values`, a file without it loads the default off = world space). Off = world space, so the new switch is off by default, as decision 24 asks of a new switch. Routable and modulatable like `view.lines` / `view.points` / `view.faces` / `view.cut_through` — the nearest `view.*` switches, all toggles left at the registry's default (`modulatable=True`); followed because it is a look switch of the same kind as `view.cut_through` (how the layer is drawn, not what is drawn), and a pad or a lane flipping stroke space at a drop is a performance move like flipping the lines. On the VIEW page (the panel's view section) under the points/lines opacity sliders, one checkbox with a tip. No APC40/pad default binding. `learn.cannot_show` (plan 24 C3) calls it dead — "points and lines off" — when both `view.points` and `view.lines` are off, as `view.lines_alpha` is with the lines off (and `_switched_on` puts it in click-to-map's "on" tier while either is on); the zoom-1 case is a rough edge of the row above, not a rule.
- **Other options:** an enum `view.stroke_space` with `world` / `screen` (reads well, but its default would be index 0 of a two-state enum rather than a switch off, and a toggle binds to the pads' toggle rows directly); a session param (it would not travel with a look, and the owner called it part of the look); not modulatable (no reason found: it holds no device or rig state).
- **To reverse:** local (the param's id lives in `params.py`, `view.py`, `ui.py`, `render.py`, the tests).
- **Rough edge:** a modulated flip mid-zoom jumps the stroke weight in one frame (no cross-fade) — the same as flipping `view.lines`.
- **Judge it:** V.A2b.1.

## 2. DECISIONS.md row draft (the toggle itself)

| Decision | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Strokes (edge width, dot radius) can be drawn in world space — scaling with `camera.zoom`, the pre-A2 feel — or in screen space — A2's fixed pixel weight — by a `view.*` switch (`view.screen_strokes`) that is part of the look. | `owner` (2026-10-14) | The owner likes strokes that render at different sizes as you are close to or far from them, and wants A2's zoom-invariant strokes as an option rather than the only behaviour. The default (world space) is register row N. |

## 4. As-built notes (shape of plan 21)

**At the machine.** Shape strokes only: the hands' tubes (world-sized, not × zoom) and A4's 1 px hand lines (drawn by `ghost.Geometry`) are the same in both modes. Every factory look at zoom ≠ 1 with lines or points draws its strokes as on `main` (before plan 26 A2): the zoomed-out fields have small dots and fine (partly faded) edges again, the zoomed-in looks slightly heavier edges. A new checkbox **Screen strokes** on the VIEW page (under the points/lines opacities) gives A2's zoom-invariant strokes. Everything at zoom 1 is unchanged.

**Code.**
- `hyperspace/view.py`: `ViewState.screen_strokes: bool = False`.
- `hyperspace/params.py`: `view.screen_strokes` toggle (group `view`, modulatable, tip), beside `view.faces`.
- `hyperspace/render.py`: `draw_scene` — `sz = 1.0 if screen_strokes else zoom` (the same clamped `max(s.zoom, 1e-3)` the model matrix takes); `width … * wscale * sz` before `draw_w = max(width, 1)` and the alpha fold; `r = (…) * dpr * sz` before the whole-pixel rounding. Module docstring updated. No shader change: the width and radius are vertex attributes already.
- `hyperspace/ui.py`: `self._check("view.screen_strokes")` on the view page after the two opacity sliders.
- `hyperspace/learn.py` (review fix 1): `cannot_show` — "points and lines off"; `_switched_on` — on while points or lines are.

**Tests.** `tests/test_stroke_space.py` (new, 7 tests): the default/group/modulatable; in a saved preset and loaded back, a file without the key lands world space; zoom 1 byte-identical in both modes (lines+points, lines only, points only, two shapes); zoom 0.5 and 2 — screen space's vertex rows identical to zoom 1, world space's width `max(raw·zoom, 1)` and alpha fold `min(raw·zoom, 1)` and radius `floor(2·raw·zoom + 0.5)/2` pinned against the formula recomputed from the shape's depths. Review fix 1: `tests/test_desk_coverage.py::test_cannot_show_is_the_rule_by_group` gains the switch's three asserts. Changed: `tests/test_camera.py::test_strokes_keep_their_weight_whatever_the_zoom` now draws with `screen_strokes=True` (A2's property is the switch's now). Goldens: 218 of 556 re-recorded (`tests/golden/frames/`; 212 on the A2b base, 6 more after merging A4/A4b). 2190 passed, 2 skipped after the merge (2140 before it).

**What no person has verified.** Everything visible: the restored weight at the machine, the checkbox's place and wording, and the feel of a zoom with strokes scaling. Judged headless from the goldens and the compact contact sheet only (no smoke run asked).

**Rough edges.** A2's goldens for zoom ≠ 1 moved a second time. A flip under a lane jumps the weight in one frame. Zoomed far in (A2's zoom up to 40×) the world-space edges get very fat — as on `main` (whose zoom was a 2D picture zoom with the same × zoom).
