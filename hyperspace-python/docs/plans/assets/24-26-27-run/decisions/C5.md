## 1. Register-row drafts

### 1xx. The grab is the four fingers' mean curl through a pinch's Gate: closes past 0.65, lets go under 0.45, 0.05 s debounce

- **Built.** `hand:<side>.grab` is 1 while the hand is a closed fist. Its
  reading is `hands.fist`: the mean of the index, middle, ring and pinky
  raw curls (C4's `curls`, unsmoothed; the thumb left out, so a fist with
  the thumb out or tucked both count). It goes through the same `Gate`
  class as the pinches: it closes once the fist passes `GRAB_CLOSE = 0.65`,
  lets go only when it falls under `GRAB_OPEN = 0.45` (a hand hovering
  half-closed does not chatter), and an edge must hold `GRAB_HOLD_S =
  0.05 s` (one noisy frame, or one frame of an open hand inside a fist, is
  ignored). A hand that leaves the box drops it at once. With no skeleton
  (a palm-only tracker) there is no reading and the grab never closes.
- **Other options.** The SDK's own `grab_strength` (one number, already
  in `features`, but opaque and not what C4's curls read); the minimum of
  the four curls instead of the mean (stricter: one badly tracked finger
  would stop a fist); the smoothed curls (adds 0.05 s lag before the
  debounce); thresholds as `hands.*` setup params like the pinches'.
- **To reverse.** Constants `GRAB_FINGERS`, `GRAB_CLOSE`, `GRAB_OPEN`,
  `GRAB_HOLD_S` in `hands/core.py` and the function `fist`: minutes.
  Swapping to `grab_strength` is one line in `step_grab`.
- **Rough edge.** Tuned on synthetic hands only: a real relaxed hand may
  idle at a fist level of 0.1–0.2 (C4's curl rough edge) and a real fist
  may stop short of 1 — 0.65 is chosen low for that. Closing the hand
  quickly also fires C4's flick and energy.
- **Judge it.** U.6, U.7.

### 1xx. The grab lanes go after C4's dance lanes; the grab is a gate after the pinches

- **Built.** Two `hand` lanes, `left.grab` and `right.grab` (labels
  "L grab", "R grab"), appended after the sixteen dance lanes: the vector
  is 40 lanes and every older lane keeps its index. `Layout.gates` (what
  the adapter publishes as `router.gates["hand"]`) lists the eight
  pinches then the two grabs, so the ROUTING page's gate combo and the
  HANDS context menu's "Gate: … ▸" offer them and a row describes as
  "… positions while R grab". Rests 0. `Layout` still builds every list
  and every count from the constants (`n`, `grab`), so stream B's B2
  reads the count, never pins it.
- **Other options.** Put the grab in `HandsIO.gates[side]` beside the
  pinches (would change the per-finger dict the ghost and the probe
  read); place the grab lanes next to the pinches (moves C4's lanes and
  every numeric address past them).
- **To reverse.** `grab_name`, the `grab` map and two list terms in
  `Layout`, the `grab` dict on `HandsIO`: an hour.
- **Rough edge.** The lane-count pins in the hand tests moved again
  (38 → 40), as C4's did.
- **Judge it.** U.6.

### 1xx. The grab has no DAW note, no setup params, and does not gate a hand Learn

- **Built.** Unlike a pinch, the grab sends no note to the DAW port, has
  no `hands.*` threshold params on the HANDS page's feel section, and a
  hand Learn done while making a fist does not write the grab as the
  row's `when` (`held_gate` still offers only a held pinch). It is picked
  as a gate on the ROUTING page or from a param's context menu.
- **Other options.** A note per grab after the pinch notes (`pinch_note`
  + 8, + 9 — would have to move the collision check); grab thresholds as
  params; `held_gate` returning a held grab, so learning `camera.yaw`
  with a fist and a turn writes the owner's mapping in one gesture —
  attractive, but it changes what an existing learn writes.
- **To reverse.** Additive each: the note in `_dances` + `_resync`, the
  params in `register`, one loop in `held_gate`.
- **Rough edge.** Authoring the grab mapping takes three ROUTING-page
  edits per row (source, target, gate) rather than a learn.
- **Judge it.** U.7.

### 1xx. The grab shows on the HANDS page as a glyph on each hand's curl row

- **Built.** In C4's dance column, after the five curl bars of each hand,

- **Other options.** Beside the pinch switches next to the box (that

- **To reverse.** `panel._grab` and three lines in `_dance`: minutes.
- **Rough edge.** Small; the fist level is in a tooltip, not on the page.
- **Judge it.** U.6.

## 2. Hand-check drafts

**U.6. The grab switch on the HANDS page and in ROUTING.**
Headless: `tests/test_hands_grab.py::{test_the_hands_page_shows_the_grab_switches,test_the_routing_page_offers_the_grab_as_a_gate,test_the_grab_lanes_follow_every_older_lane,test_the_grab_lane_closes_with_a_fist_and_holds_through_noise}`.
Hands on (camera or `[hands] tape`). HANDS page: each hand's curl row in
the dance column ends in "grab ○". Open hand; slowly close it into a
fist; hold; loosen it halfway; open it; flash a fist for a split second;
take the hand out while it is a fist. ROUTING: open a row's gate combo.
*Pass:* "grab ●" lights red once the fist is made (hover: fist ≥ 0.65),
stays lit while loosened halfway, goes dark when open; a split-second
fist does nothing; a fist leaving the box goes dark at once; the gate
combo lists "L grab" and "R grab" after the eight pinches. *Fail:* never
lights on a real fist → hover the glyph and read the fist level; if it
tops out under 0.65, lower `GRAB_CLOSE`; lights on a relaxed open hand →
raise it; flickers while half-closed → widen the gap to `GRAB_OPEN`;
never lights at all and the curl bars are hollow → the tracker gives no
skeleton.

**U.7. The owner's grab mapping (grab to steer the shape).**
Headless: `tests/test_hands_grab.py::{test_the_shape_follows_the_hand_only_while_it_is_closed,test_the_fixture_is_the_owners_grab_mapping}`.
Into a scratch preset (don't save over yours), on the ROUTING page add
three `position` rows: `hand:right.yaw` → `camera.yaw`, `hand:right.pitch`
→ `camera.pitch`, `hand:right.roll` → `rot.phase.XY`, each with gate
"R grab". Open hand: turn it about. Then make a fist and turn it; open
it again mid-turn. *Pass:* the open hand moves nothing; with a fist the
shape turns with the hand; opening lets go and the shape returns to
where its params sit. *Fail:* the shape jumps when the fist closes → that
is `position` (absolute) by design — the owner decides whether the grab
should instead catch the shape where it is (a relative op, plan 28's
territory); the turn feels 2:1 → the route's depth/scale (the axis's
range maps onto the param's whole throw).

## 3. As-built notes (shape of `21-outputs-and-the-hands.md`)

**At the machine.** A fist lights "grab ●" on the HANDS page; "L grab" /
"R grab" appear in every gate picker. Nothing moves until a route is gated
on it: no preset uses it (decision 23). SETUP.md's projector example says
`hands = true`.

**Code.**
- `hands/core.py`: constants `GRAB_FINGERS`, `GRAB_CLOSE`, `GRAB_OPEN`,
  `GRAB_HOLD_S`; `fist(curls)`; `step_grab(gate, curls, t)` (feeds a
  `Gate` 1 − fist so it closes past `GRAB_CLOSE`); `grab_name`;
  `addresses` gains the two grab names last; `Layout.grab`, two names,
  and two `gates` entries after the pinches; `HandsIO.grab` (a `Gate`
  per side), stepped in `_dances` from `Dance.raw["curl"]`, reset when
  the hand is away; `lanes()` fills the grab lanes; `_grabs()` and
  `_layout()` rebuild what an object from before a hot reload lacks.
  Still no host import.
- `hands/core.py` (C4 review): `Dance.step` sets the energy target to 0
  on a fresh frame that measures no speed.
- `hands/__init__.py`: exports `fist`, `grab_name`.
- `hands/panel.py`: `_grab` (the glyph and its tooltip) on each curl
  row; `_axes` puts the cursor under the taller of the axis rows and the
  dance column and emits an empty item (C4 review).
- `docs/SETUP.md`: the projector example's `hands = true` and a sentence.

**Tests.** `tests/test_hands_grab.py` (11): the fist reading, the
Schmitt + debounce, the layout (no older lane moves, gates after the
pinches, counts derived), a tape of a right hand closing, hovering,
blinking open and leaving (edges at exactly the debounced frames), a
palm-only source never grabs, the fixture preset
(`tests/fixtures/grab-follow/grab-follow.json`, force-added: yaw → camera.yaw,
pitch → camera.pitch, roll → rot.phase.XY, each `when hand:right.grab`)
loaded in a scratch home and played from the tape — every row shut while
open, acting while closed, the plane following the roll, letting go on
open and on leaving, no base moved; the ROUTING gate combo and the row's
description; the HANDS page glyph inside the width; a two-row `dofs`
setup whose editor starts below the dance column. `tests/test_hands_dance.py`
gains the energy test. `tests/fake_hands.py` gains `close=` (a
half-closed hand; defaults unchanged).

**What no person has verified.** The thresholds on real LMC2 fists; that
a real relaxed hand reads well under 0.45; the glyph's legibility at
distance; the mapping's feel (absolute `position` jumps on grab).

**Rough edges.** `position` is absolute, so grabbing snaps the shape to
the hand's pose rather than catching it where it is — "follows 1:1" as
the owner described may want a relative op (plan 28); no DAW note for
the grab; Learn does not attach it.
