# C4 — inputs that reward dancing (H2): decisions, hand checks, as-built

Branch `plan-24-c4-dance-sources`, base `cd9106d`. Owner's decisions honoured:
9 and 20 (the twelve axes stay; the H2 sources added beside them), 23 (no
preset edited or added; the proof is a fixture under `tests/fixtures/`),

## 1. Register-row drafts

### 1xx. The dance sources' lanes go after the pinch switches; `hands:` is its own scheme

- **Built.** Each hand gets eight new `hand:` lanes — `curl.thumb` …
  `curl.pinky`, `spread`, `energy`, `flick` — appended **after** the
  eight pinch switches, so the `hand` vector is 38 lanes and every older
  lane keeps its index (a saved `hand:9` still reads the right roll).
  `hands:gap` and `hands:twist` are a second scheme, `hands`, with its own
  vector, labels (`gap`, `twist`), name map, rests (0, 0.5) and an empty
  gate list, fed every frame beside `hand`. `Layout` builds both lists.
  The word `hands` is added to `routing.LANE_SCHEMES` and
  `ui_routing.SOURCE_ORDER` and nothing else. Of the per-hand lanes only
  the pinches (and, from C5, the grab) are gates — published in
  `gates["hand"]`, offered as a row's `when`; a flick is not a gate (it
  spikes and decays, it is never held), nor is any other dance source.
- **Other options.** Put the pair lanes in the `hand` vector too
  (`hand:both.gap`) — no new scheme, but the plan names `hands:`; put the
  new per-hand lanes before the switches — breaks every numeric
  `hand:<i>` past the axes.
- **To reverse.** The lane order is one function (`addresses`) and the
  `Layout` constructor; a preset names lanes by name, so a reorder costs
  nothing but numeric addresses. Dropping `hands` is the two tuple words
  plus `pair_lanes`/`_publish`: an hour.
- **Rough edge.** Nine existing hand-test assertions pinned the vector's
  length or its tail (`22`, `[14:]`); they were changed to 38 / `[14:22]`
  (see the PR body). B2's device wrapper must read the count from
  `Layout`, never pin it.
- **Judge it.** U.1, U.5.

### 1xx. Curl is the summed bone-to-bone bend; spread is the index–pinky fan in the palm's plane

- **Built.** `curl.<digit>` = the angles between each bone and the next
  (zero-length bones skipped — the thumb's metacarpal), summed, mapped
  20°→0 … 220°→1 for the fingers and 10°→0 … 80°→1 for the thumb.
  `spread` = the angle between the index's and the pinky's proximal
  bones, both projected into the palm plane, 10°→0 … 60°→1; a finger
  bent more than ~70° at the knuckle (in-plane length under 0.35 of the
  bone) makes the spread read 0 (a fist does not fan). Both smoothed by a
  one-pole slew, 0.05 s each way. Both read the skeleton, so a source
  with no skeleton gives NaN (no reading), not a fake 0. Per-digit raw
  curls are `hands.curls(skel)` and `HandsIO.dance[side].raw["curl"]`,
  smoothed `HandsIO.dance[side].curl[digit].y` — what C5's grab reads.
- **Other options.** The SDK's `grab_strength` for curl (one number per
  hand, not per digit); fingertip-to-palm distance over palm width
  (hand-size independent but conflates curl with spread); tip-to-tip
  distance for spread (curling the fingers then reads as "closing").
- **To reverse.** The ranges are constants in `hands/core.py`
  (`CURL_RANGE`, `CURL_RANGE_FINGER`, `SPREAD_RANGE`, `SPREAD_MIN_FLAT`,
  `CURL_S`): minutes.
- **Rough edge.** Tuned on synthetic hands only. A real relaxed open hand
  bends 20–40° in total, so curls may idle a little above 0; a real fist
  may not reach 220°. Spread measures only the index–pinky angle: a
  middle finger crossing its neighbours is invisible.
- **Judge it.** U.2, U.3.

### 1xx. Energy is the mean speed of the palm and the five tips, fast up, slow down

- **Built.** On each new tracking frame, the speed of the palm and each
  fingertip (mm/s, from the frame's receipt times), averaged; 60 mm/s →
  0 (a floor above a still hand's jitter) … 1000 mm/s → 1; then a slew
  with attack 0.06 s and decay 0.5 s, so a burst of dancing lingers.
  Without a skeleton the palm alone. A returning hand starts at 0 (its
  first frame measures no speed); frames more than 0.1 s apart measure
  nothing.
- **Other options.** Peak instead of mean (one finger dominates); the
  palm only (misses finger work); a longer window (One Euro, an RMS).
- **To reverse.** Constants `ENERGY_RANGE`, `ENERGY_ATTACK_S`,
  `ENERGY_DECAY_S`, `SPEED_GAP_S`: minutes.
- **Rough edge.** The floor is a guess at the LMC2's jitter at 90–120 Hz;
  if a still hand reads above 0, raise it.
- **Judge it.** U.2.

### 1xx. A flick is the fastest tip crossing 1500 mm/s; it spikes and decays with τ = 0.15 s

- **Built.** Until stream B's B2 gives triggers a signal kind,
  `hand:<side>.flick` is a continuous lane: 0 at rest; when the fastest
  fingertip's speed (the palm's with no skeleton) reaches 1500 mm/s it
  jumps to the hit's strength, speed / 3000 mm/s (at most 1), keeps the
  peak while the move lasts, then decays as e^(−t/0.15 s). Schmitt: no
  new hit until the speed has fallen under 700 mm/s. A speed over
  10000 mm/s is a tracking jump and ignored. A hand entering the box
  never flicks on its first frame. (`signal.Flick`.)
- **Other options.** Acceleration instead of speed (noisier at 90 Hz);
  fingertips relative to the palm (finger flicks only — a whole-hand
  snap would not count); a hold-then-drop gate shape instead of a decay.
- **To reverse.** Constants `FLICK_ON`, `FLICK_OFF`, `FLICK_FULL`,
  `FLICK_CEILING`, `FLICK_DECAY_S`: minutes. When B2 lands a trigger kind,
  the lane becomes a hit with velocity = the strength.
- **Rough edge.** Any fast move is a flick — closing a fist quickly, a
  fast hand pass — so energy and flick fire together on big moves. The
  Leap occasionally snaps a fingertip a few cm in one frame; below the
  ceiling that reads as a flick.
- **Judge it.** U.4.

### 1xx. Gap is the palms' distance; twist is the right hand's roll less the left's

- **Built.** `hands:gap` = the distance between the palms, 60 mm → 0 …
  600 mm → 1. `hands:twist` = right roll − left roll (`features`'
  anatomical roll, so mirror-image hands — both palms down, a clap —
  read 0.5), wrapped to ±180°, ±90° → 0 … 1; turning an imaginary wheel
  clockwise (as the performer sees it) reads up. Both slewed 0.05 s; both
  NaN unless both hands are in. Rests: gap 0, twist 0.5.
- **Other options.** Twist as the full relative rotation (roll, pitch and
  yaw together, an unsigned angle) — loses the direction; room-relative
  roll difference — then a clap reads ±180° and wraps.
- **To reverse.** `GAP_RANGE`, `TWIST_FULL`, `PAIR_S`, `PAIR_RESTS`, and
  the two functions: an hour.
- **Rough edge.** Twist reads roll only: two hands pitching against each
  other do not twist. At ±90° each (a quarter wheel each way) it wraps.
- **Judge it.** U.5.

### 1xx. The dance sources have no setup params, no DAW output and no hand Learn

- **Built.** None of the new sources is a registry param (no per-source
  dead zone, curve, attack, rest), none goes to the DAW MIDI port, and a
  hand Learn still lands only an axis — the dance sources are picked on
  the ROUTING page (source combo: `hand R curl ind`, `hands gap`).
- **Other options.** Each as a full `AxisSettings` axis (a dozen params
  each, MIDI out, Learn): far more surface for sources no preset uses yet.
- **To reverse.** Additive: give them settings later.
- **Rough edge.** Their feel is fixed in code until the owner asks; a
  route's `motion` (route motion, PR #162) can still shape one per row.
- **Judge it.** U.1.

### 1xx. The HANDS page shows the dance sources in a column beside the axis rows

- **Built.** A 220 px column to the right of the twelve axis rows (the
  axis bars are that much shorter): per hand a row of five curl bars and
  a row of spread / energy / flick, then gap and twist (twist with a
  middle tick). Hollow while a source reads nothing; hover for its address
  and value. Beside the rows, not under them, because the page is
  already exactly the control window's height (the fit tests).
- **Other options.** A folded "dance sources" header (still one line too
  many when the ghost shows every line); beside the box and switches
  (no room at 960 px).
- **To reverse.** `panel._dance` and two lines in `_axes`: minutes.
- **Rough edge.** The bars are small (16 px); the axis bars lost 220 px.
- **Judge it.** U.1.

## 2. Hand-check drafts

**U.1. The dance sources on the HANDS page and in ROUTING.**
Headless: `tests/test_hands_dance.py::{test_the_hands_page_shows_every_new_source,test_the_routing_page_offers_the_new_sources_by_name,test_the_new_lanes_follow_the_switches_and_no_older_lane_moves}`.

axis rows. Hands out of the box; then one hand in; then both. ROUTING:
open a row's source combo. *Pass:* bars hollow with no hand, filled for
the hand in view, gap and twist only with both; hovering shows
`hand:right.curl.index: 0.xxx`; the combo lists `hand L curl thu` …
`hand R flick` and `hands gap`, `hands twist`; the twelve axes read as

**U.2. Curl and energy feel.**
Headless: `tests/test_hands_dance.py::{test_an_open_hand_is_uncurled_and_a_fist_is_curled,test_every_new_source_moves_its_param_through_a_scratch_library}`.
Route `hand:right.curl.index` → `view.bright` (position) and
`hand:right.energy` → `view.speed` on the ROUTING page (into a scratch
preset, don't save over yours). Open hand still; slowly curl the index
alone; make a fist; shake the hand; hold still. *Pass:* an open, relaxed
hand reads near 0 on every curl, a fist near 1, the index's curl moves
alone first (the others follow a little — anatomy); energy near 0 when
still, high while shaking, falling over about a second after. *Fail:*
open hand well above 0 → raise `CURL_RANGE_FINGER`'s low end; fist under
~0.8 → lower its high end; still hand shows energy → raise
`ENERGY_RANGE`'s floor.

**U.3. Spread.**
Headless: `tests/test_hands_dance.py::test_spread_is_the_fan_and_a_fist_does_not_fan`.
Palm down, fingers together, then fanned wide, then a fist. *Pass:*
together ≈ 0, fanned wide ≈ 1, a fist 0. *Fail:* wide never reaches 1 →
lower `SPREAD_RANGE`'s high end; together reads > 0.2 → raise its low end.

**U.4. Flick.**
Headless: `tests/test_hands_dance.py::{test_a_flick_spikes_to_its_strength_holds_its_peak_and_decays,test_every_new_source_moves_its_param_through_a_scratch_library}`.
Route `hand:right.flick` → `view.bright` (push, depth 1). Flick the
fingers sharply, softly, and move the hand slowly across the box. *Pass:*
a sharp flick flashes and fades in a few tenths of a second, a harder one
brighter; a slow pass does nothing; one flick, one flash. *Fail:* nothing
on a real flick → lower `FLICK_ON`; flashes on ordinary moves → raise it;
double flashes → raise `FLICK_OFF`'s gap.

**U.5. Gap and twist.**
Headless: `tests/test_hands_dance.py::{test_twist_reads_level_for_mirror_hands_and_up_for_a_clockwise_wheel,test_gap_is_the_palms_distance}`.
Route `hands:gap` → `camera.zoom`, `hands:twist` → `camera.yaw`. Both
hands in: bring them together and apart; hold a steering wheel and turn
it clockwise and back; clap position (palms facing). *Pass:* gap follows
the distance smoothly, ≈ 0 with palms nearly touching, ≈ 1 at arm's
width; twist level with mirror hands and in the clap, up when turning
clockwise, down anticlockwise; one hand out → both let go. *Fail:* twist
runs backwards → the sign in `core.twist`; gap saturates early → widen
`GAP_RANGE`.

## 3. As-built notes (shape of `21-outputs-and-the-hands.md`)

**At the machine.** Nothing is new until a route names a source: no
preset uses them (decision 23). On the HANDS page a column beside the
axes shows them live. In ROUTING the source combo offers each by name.

**Code.**
- `hands/signal.py`: `unit`, `Smooth` (a slew that starts on its first
  reading and forgets on None), `Flick` (Schmitt on a speed, spike to
  strength, peak hold, e^(−t/τ) decay, ceiling for tracking jumps).
- `hands/core.py`: the constants block under `DANCE` (ranges and time
  constants); geometry `bend`, `curls`, `spread`, `points`, `gap`,
  `twist`; `Dance` (one hand's run-time state); `addresses` gains the
  eight per-hand lanes after the switches; `pair_addresses`; `Layout`
  gains `dance`, `pair_names`, `pair_addresses`, `pair_index`;
  `HandsIO.dance` / `.pair`, `_dances` (stepped each poll after the
  skeletons), `lanes()` fills the dance lanes, `pair_lanes`,
  `pair_rests`, `_layout` (rebuilds a pre-reload layout), `_publish`
  publishes the `hands` scheme, `poll` feeds `hands` beside `hand`.
  Still no host import (boundary tests untouched but the count).
- `hands/__init__.py`: exports `DANCE`, `PAIR`, `Dance`, `curls`,
  `spread`, `pair_addresses`.
- `hands/panel.py`: `_dance` / `_dance_bar`; `_axes` reserves `DANCE_W`.
- `routing.LANE_SCHEMES` and `ui_routing.SOURCE_ORDER` gain `hands`.

**Tests.** `tests/test_hands_dance.py` (17): shaping, geometry, layout,
publication, a fixture preset (`tests/fixtures/dance-sources/
dance-sources.json`, force-added) loaded into a scratch home and played
from a saved synthetic tape — every one of the 18 sources moves its
param, every row lets go when the hands leave, no base moves; the
ROUTING combo; a palm-only source; the HANDS page draws all 18 bars
inside the window. `tests/fake_hands.py` gains `spread` fanning and
`roll` (defaults unchanged). Changed assertions: see the PR body.

**What no person has verified.** Every range and time constant on real
LMC2 hands; that the skeleton's bones give curls near 0/1 for a real
open hand / fist; the flick threshold against real flicks and the Leap's
fingertip jumps; the panel column's legibility at distance.

**Rough edges.** Flick and energy both fire on any fast move; twist is
roll only; spread is index–pinky only; the dance sources have no
per-source settings; no MIDI to the DAW for them.

## 4. What the documents say that the code does not do

- The prompt says a flick "is a trigger kind in stream B's model": not
  built here (B1/B2's); the lane spikes and decays, as the prompt allows.
- `ENGINEERING.md` / `ARCHITECTURE.md` describe the hand vector as axes,
  presence, pinches (22 lanes) — the docs WP should add the 16 dance
  lanes after the switches and the `hands` scheme.
- `tests/test_hands_boundary.py:71` was the one change the prompt
  allowed; eight more assertions in `tests/test_hands.py` (lines 408,
  410, 940, 958, 965, 1120, 1180, 1187, 1199) and one in
  `tests/test_hand_names.py` (129) also pinned the 22-lane count or the
  switches being the vector's tail. Each was changed only for the count
  (22 → 38, `[14:]` → `[14:22]`, `[0.0] * 10` → `[0.0] * 26`, the feed
  count read per scheme). No other line of any existing test changed.
- Not done: Learn by moving a dance source; DAW MIDI out for them.
