# Glossary

**Signed by the owner, 2026-11-06,** after review with Dillon: "we reviewed the glossary,
and we're good to go". One row per concept, in the owner's words, from
`plans/wave-4-session-prompt.md`: "The semantic migration" and its rounds of answers.

New code uses these words now. Old names stay until SEM renames them, once; no PR
renames in passing. **Code in SEM, files in the format lane:** a saved key or id is
a format move and rides the one migration. If it cannot change cheaply it stays, and the
last column says so.

There are two layers and no others. **The meta controller** is everything set for a
performance or an install. **The patch** is where anything can change anything; a
**loadable patch** has no wires coming out, a complete look you can play. Neither layer
may leak into the other, and no loadable patch can affect another loadable patch. (A patch
wired into its host does drive it: that is what its wires are for, inside one look.)

| Word | What it is | The code's old names it replaces | Does a saved name change? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **meta controller** | Everything set or tuned for a performance or an install, and not actively changed during one. Owns the two `Deck`s and says which `Deck` each controller, the keys, the mouse and the panel are on. In code: `deck.MetaController` | `session` (`SessionRegistry`, `SessionTable`, `part_of == "session"`), `rig` (`RigWires`, `RigMade`, `Presets.rig`), the show (`Project`, `*.hyp.json`), lowercase "deck" in prose | **Yes, for shows** (owner, 2026-11-06, FQ5, `plans/57-format-lane.md` §5): show files go; tuning and the bank move to `<library>/installs/<name>.json`, in the one migration; the panel says install (row 850). `config.toml`'s words, `config.toml.rig` and the venues of #223 are still open: wave 5 |
| **patch** | The general form: a piece of graph where anything can change anything, with wires that may come out of it to plug into something else | "performance" (`part_of == "performance"`, `PERFORMANCE_GROUPS`), "group not yet saved". The code already reads a patch and a preset as one shape (plan 50) | No: `patches/` and `hyperspace.patch/…` already say it. `part_of`'s string is not saved. In a file, the schema's kind decides which it is (`patches.shape_of`), not a count of wires |
| **loadable patch** | A patch with no wires coming out: a complete visual look you can play, with its values, graph, routes and desk layer | "preset", everywhere, and "look" in prose where it means the file | **Yes** (owner, 2026-11-06, FQ4): `presets/` becomes `loadable/` and the schema `hyperspace.loadable/11`, in the one migration |
| **`Deck`** | A loadable patch once loaded, inside the meta controller: the objects one loadable patch runs on (`deck.Deck`). There are exactly two. "Slot" is not a type: on air and off air say which `Deck` | `Deck` stays. "Deck 1", "deck 2" in comments stay until SEM's words pass; lowercase "deck" meaning the rig goes (row one). `Decks`, plan 44's name, was never built | None saved |
| **on air** | The `Deck` the outputs show. Whichever the crossfader is closer to. In code: `MetaController.on_air` | "program", "the deck on air", `Fade.on_air`, `session.lead` | None saved. `output.hold`, `.take`, `.drop` go in D5d |
| **off air** | The other `Deck`: built, explored and loaded into without the room seeing it. In code: `MetaController.off_air` | "preview", "held", `Host.off_air`, `Fade.off_air`, `MediaIO.off_air` | None saved |
| **player** | A named actor, player 1 to 4, who registers any number of controllers and holds a duty. A patch names a player, never a device. In code from PL: `Player` | Plan 27's roles (`axis@p2`) are the seam. Bare `pad:` and `axis:` rows read as player 1 | None today: bare rows stay as they are, with no file change. Node mode's saved player names arrived with #377, as an optional key with no bump |
| **duty** | What a player is on: on air or off air. It stays until someone moves the player. In code from PL: `Duty` | Nothing yet | None |
| **controller** | One physical input a player holds: the APC40, a pad, a Joy-Con, the hands. Generic: what it does comes from rows, not from what it is. In code, one device (`devices.Base`) with its own `Pointer`, keyed by role | "device" for an input, "desk" for the APC40 (`desk.*` ids stay), "pad" where it means any of them | `[[devices]]` and `role` in `config.toml`, and `desk.*` ids: no, they stay. NSEW renames only the buttons (`pad:N`, `hat:N`, `pair:N/S`; owner, 2026-11-06, FQ7), in the format lane |
| **analyzer** | What listens to the room and makes the lanes every patch may wire from: a fixture on the graph. Its tuning is the meta controller's; what plugs into it is the patch's. In code `analyzer.py` already says it | `band` in words (336 uses), the "bands" pages. A band is still one of its frequency ranges | **No** (owner, 2026-11-06, FQ3): `band.*` ids and the `band` group stay, since "band" is right for one frequency range. The words changed in AZ |
| **node mode** | A controller pointed at one node of a patch, its degrees of freedom filled by that node's params. The controller's choice, entered from the panel; a cable of its own kind and colour, saved in the loadable patch | Nothing yet. Plan 53's "temporary cables" and "keep this one" are gone | Yes, already: `node_mode` holds player, controller and node, as an optional key with no bump (#377). The cable kind is derived, not saved |
| **crossfade** | Moving the outputs between the two `Deck`s, as a DJ mixer's crossfader: the APC's crossfader. Fading into an empty `Deck` is black. One of two verbs left, with "copy on air to off air"; a timed fade is a control on it. In code `Fade`, `Mix`, `output.xfade` | TAKE, DROP, HOLD (`hold.py`, gone in D5d), "swap" (there is none), "the mix", "the take" | `output.xfade` and `output.xfade_s` keep their ids: never saved in a patch. A learned row on one stays |
| **dim** | The master level of the whole picture, the meta controller's: the APC's MASTER fader from D5d. Separate from the crossfade | "the crossfader" as it reads on the desk today (the desk's crossfader row goes to `output.xfade` in D5d) | `output.dim` keeps its id |
| **field** | A value, or a pair, at every pixel: what something reads to vary across the picture — an offset, a time, a knob's spread. Its own wire (owner, 2026-11-22), never a picture, seen as grey. Plan 67 | "the field" for the copies' layout, which is the **lattice** (owner, 2026-11-23) | No: no saved id holds the word (`view.inst_*`; Grow's and Wave Table's knob is `mode`). Labels, code (`field.py` → `lattice.py`) and prose |
| **rides along** | What a field carries in its spare channels for the next op, unseen when it is shown: a Cells field's cell size beside its number, its side of a pair beside the place in the cell. Waveform passes it on. Plan 67 §14 | "metadata", "extra channels" | No: a field is never saved |
| **point** | A place on the frame as a field node: every pixel's offset from it, in frame heights. Line, Rings, Spokes, Cells and Noise count from the Point wired into their `field` port (the middle with none), so one Point moves them all. Plan 67 §15 | "Centre X / Y" on a field node (the effects keep theirs until they take a field) | Yes: `fx.point.x`, `fx.point.y` |
| **lattice** | Where each copy of the shape sits: grid, hex, ring and the 3D lattices. In code `field.py` until it is renamed `lattice.py` | "the instance field", "the field", "Field motion / amp / rate / depth" on the panel | No: the params stay `view.inst_*` |
